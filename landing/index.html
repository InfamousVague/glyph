Organise

A place for everything, in plain Markdown.

Every note is a .md file you own. Gather them as your life needs.

  • Home

    Pinned first, then where you left off.

  • Journals

    Dated entries, from your own template.

  • Boards

    To-dos as cards, still lines in the note.

  • Canvases

    Ideas on a page, joined up.

  • Find anything. Search every word, and filter by notebook, pinned or workspace.
  • Keep work apart. Workspaces hold their own notes, each in its colour.
  • Tidy by feel. Swipe to pin or archive, further to delete. Pull down to sync.

Plan

Tickets, boards and queries, written in Markdown.

A few plain marks, drawn as the tools a team plans with. The file stays text: any Markdown app opens it, and Obsidian and GitHub read the same marks.

  • To-dos that carry their details

    A person, a priority and a due day on any to-do, as Obsidian Tasks writes them, drawn as chips you tap to change.

    - [ ] Renew the insurance @matt ⏫ 📅 2026-10-02

  • Tickets

    A note whose front matter says it is a ticket. Its fields become a panel at the top, and a notebook with a key numbers them.

    ---
type: ticket
id: PRD-11
status: In progress
assignee: Sam
priority: highest
---

  • Queries

    What matches across every note, as a table, a list, a board, a month, a timeline or a count. Totals add up as you go.

    ```query
from: tickets [[Product]]
where: status != Done
sort: priority, due
total: estimate
```

  • Boards

    Named items laid out in columns. Drag a card to move it, and the line under the board sets how tall it is.

    ```board
To do: ship-page
Done: pick-date
```
  • Choices. ( ) and (x) on a list: tap one to pick it and clear the rest.
  • Sums. A line starting = shows its answer: = $45 + $18 * 2 → $81.
  • Blanks for the AI. {?what time does it open?} is a question the phone's AI answers in place, when you ask it to.

Every mark, with an example to copy, is on the formatting reference. The app's Academy teaches them one at a time, by typing each.

Work with AI

AI that works in your notes, not instead of them.

  • On your phone. Format, Summarize or Enhance a note with a model that runs on the device. Nothing leaves it.
  • Every change marked. What the AI adds is tinted and what it removes is struck through, each with Keep and Revert.
  • Claude, by name. Claude's connector reads, searches, writes and adds to your notes, and signs what it wrote. It never deletes, and never writes over an edit from your other devices.
  • Your tools. List items become Notion tasks or GitHub issues, and a tick stays in step both ways.

Connected

Claude and Notion, working in the same notes.

Ask Claude to run the board.

Connect Claude to Ghost.md and it reads, searches and writes your notes, tickets included. Ask it in words:

  • Make a ticket for the login loop, highest priority, for Sam.A ticket note, its fields in front matter, in the notebook you name.
  • Move PRD-11 to In review and give it a due day.It finds the ticket by its key and changes its fields.
  • What is due this week, and who has it?It searches your tickets and to-dos and tells you.
  • Add a board of the sprint to my planning note.It writes the query, and the app draws the board.

Claude signs what it writes, and joins the note's authors. It never deletes a note, and never writes over a change made on your other devices.

Your to-dos, as Notion tasks.

Sign in with Notion and pick a board. Then a to-do in any note can become a task there:

  • One at a time, or a whole list. Swipe an item, tap the quiet Notion after it, or send the note's list from its More sheet.
  • In step both ways. Tick the item and the task moves; change the task in Notion and its status shows on the item.
  • Open it in place. Tap a linked item to see the task in a drawer, without leaving the note.
  • GitHub too. A note linked to a repo sends its items as issues, ticked both ways the same.

Document

Meetings, written up for you.

Record with the screen off. Afterwards the phone transcribes it and writes a summary under the title: what was decided, and the to-dos. The tape stays with the note, and on the phone.

Private

Yours, and only yours.

  • No account needed. Without one, nothing of yours is on a server.
  • Sealed before it syncs. Notes, tapes and pictures are encrypted on the device.
  • No ads, no analytics. The microphone is on only while you record.

Connecting Claude through our hosted connector lets it read your notes while connected; run the connector on your own computer to keep your key there. The privacy policy
