Ghost.md

version2.3.94formatCommonMark + front mattersyncend-to-end sealedaccountoptionalplatforms5plugins22

Notes, journals, boards, canvases, notebooks and a team's tickets, kept as plain Markdown on your own devices and sealed when they sync. Claude, your calendar, Notion, GitHub and Slack are a switch away. No account needed.

Install

  • Androidglyph.apk
  • iPhoneApp Store
  • Macglyph.dmg, 13.1+
  • Windowsglyph-setup.exe
  • Browserattack.fm/glyph

The downloads are the release's own files; the app updates itself from the same place. Connect Claude Code in one line:

claude mcp add --transport http glyph https://ghostmarkdown.com/api/mcp

What it is

  • Every note is a .md file, named after its title, in a folder you own. Journals, boards, canvases, notebooks and tickets are Markdown too, so Obsidian opens them and GitHub renders them.
  • Sealed sync. With an account, notes, tapes and pictures are encrypted on the device, end to end. Without one, nothing of yours is on a server.
  • Claude built in with your own key, and a connector for Claude's own apps.
  • Voice. Speak a note, record a meeting with the phone locked; the device transcribes and summarises.
  • Versions kept like git, in a file beside the note.
  • Teams. Organizations, live cursors, comments, tickets, cycles, a Gantt, forms, rules.

Marks

A few plain marks on top of Markdown, drawn as tools. The file stays text. Every mark, with an example to copy, is on the formatting reference.

To-dos that carry their details

- [ ] Renew the insurance @matt ⏫ 📅 2026-10-02

A person, a priority and a due day, as Obsidian Tasks writes them, drawn as chips you tap to change. A due day lands on your calendar; a place reminds you when you get there (Android).

Claude

In the app with your own Anthropic key, kept in the device's secure storage: ask in the chat and the note opens as Claude starts writing, word by word at its own caret, its thinking shown. Talk to it from the dock. Android, iPhone and the Mac.

Through the connector from claude.ai, Claude Desktop and Claude Code: it reads, searches and writes notes, tickets and comments, shows as online while it works, signs what it writes, and never writes over an edit from another device.

https://ghostmarkdown.com/api/mcp
"Make a ticket for the login loop, highest priority, for Sam." → a ticket note, its fields in front matter, in the notebook you name.

Calendar and meetings

  • Google Calendar: today's events at the head of home; a to-do with a due day is an all-day event within seconds, moves when the day changes, goes when ticked off.
  • Any iCal address (iCloud, Outlook, Fastmail): events in, nothing to sign in to. Only events Ghost.md made are ever changed.
  • Events you can query: from: events, shown as a list, table, month or timeline.
  • Meetings: invite you@in.ghostmarkdown.com to a Meet or Zoom call, or paste a link. Every occurrence of a series. Transcript with speakers; summary written on your device; filed into the right workspace's Meetings folder.

Privacy

  • No account needed; without one, nothing of yours is on a server.
  • Notes, tapes and pictures are encrypted on the device, end to end. A team's notes with a key only its members hold.
  • No ads, no analytics, no trackers, in the app or on this site.
  • Export everything as one .ghostex file, or just copy the folder.
  • Connecting Claude through the hosted connector lets it read your notes while connected; run the connector on your own computer to keep your key there. The privacy policy.
