Claude

Ask, and watch it write.

Claude lives in the app with your own key, and writes into your notes as it thinks. Or connect Claude's own apps and let them read and write your notes, live.

For teams

Tickets, cycles and a Gantt, in files the team owns.

Give a notebook a key and its notes become tickets. Boards, a Gantt and a roadmap are drawn from a query, and all of it stays Markdown.

Calendar

Your day, on one page.

Connect Google Calendar and today's events sit at the head of home, above your notes. A to-do with a due day is on your calendar seconds later.

Meetings

Ghost goes to your meetings.

Invite it to a Meet or Zoom call and it joins by itself, writes the transcript as the call runs, and files the summary as a note with the to-dos as to-dos.

Plain Markdown

Files you own, all the way down.

Journals, boards, canvases, notebooks and tickets are .md files in a folder. Open them in Obsidian or on GitHub, and back them up with git.

  • Sealed before it syncs

    Encrypted on the device, end to end. The service can't read a word.

  • Every version, kept like git

    A timeline of who changed what, beside the note, in a file you can send.

  • Plugins, when you want them

    Notion, Slack, GitHub, Todoist, Lovart and more, each a switch in the Market.
