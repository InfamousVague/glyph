Notes

Format
CommonMark with front matter; one .md file per note, named after its title
Kinds
Notes, journals (dated entries, any number a day, your template), boards, canvases (.canvas, JSON Canvas 1.0), notebooks (notes in order, with timelines and diagrams from their lines), tickets
Home
Pinned first, then recent notes as cards, today's events at the head, a shelf of recordings
Search
Every word; filters by notebook, pinned, workspace
Workspaces
Each its own folder and colour; organizations add shared ones
Links
[[wiki links]], backlinks, embeds, folding, @mentions of people and days
Templates
For notes, journals and new tickets; snippets
Versions
Kept beside the note as .versions: who, when, the lines out and in; every fiftieth a whole copy, each hashed; restore any, undo the restore; merged across devices
Comments
On a few words or a line, threads, resolve; kept in the note's own Markdown

Diagrams
```mermaid fences drawn in place; Gantts as their own mark
Tables
Drawn and edited in place; +Col, +Row
Embeds

Marks plugin
A spoiler in smoke, highlights, asides, redactions, effects; Ghost's own on top of Markdown
Reference
Every mark, with an example to copy; the Academy teaches them one at a time

Plan

Tickets
Front matter type: ticket; a notebook with a key numbers them (HT-12); status, assignee, due, priority, estimate, labels, components, cycle, epic, milestone, blocked_by, sub-tickets with a progress bar
Queries
```query: from, where, sort, group, total, show: list | table | board | calendar | timeline | gantt | chart | rollup
Boards
```board of named items in columns; drag to move; or a query shown as a board, with bulk edits and one Undo
Gantt
Drag to move or stretch, link what waits on what, milestones, today's line, Week / Month / Quarter; old Mermaid gantts convert in a tap
Cycles
Burndown, velocity, estimates, unfinished work carried to the next
Epics, roadmap
Epics add up their tickets; a roadmap drawn from milestones
Views
My tickets (by due day, across notebooks); Triage by keys J K A P T; watchers; history; a morning digest; duplicates shown as a ticket is made; Merge into… with Undo
Rules
```rules sentences run as tickets change: "When status becomes Done, post to Slack"
Forms
A link anyone can fill with no account; each answer arrives sealed as a ticket
Time
Start / Stop timers on tickets; a GitHub pull request on its ticket

Teams

Organizations
Invite by handle or link; each member gets the organization's workspace; shared notebooks and folders
Encryption
A key only members hold; the service can't read a title, a word, a comment or a cursor
Live
Typing reaches the others as typed; carets in each member's colour with their name; who is here and what they are editing; Jump to cursor
Verified pages
Mark a page as the team's current truth, by whom, until when
Audit
Every change to every note, by who; the team's news beside it
Sharing
Read-only share links at ghostmarkdown.com/read; sealed share metas; published sites

Claude

In the app
Your own Anthropic key in the device's secure storage; chat from the dock; the note opens and Claude writes at its own caret, word by word; its thinking shown; follow along or take over; talk to it (Android, Mac)
Connector
https://ghostmarkdown.com/api/mcp for claude.ai, Claude Desktop and Claude Code; reads, searches and writes notes, tickets and comments; create_note from a template; shows as online; signs what it writes; never writes over another device's edit; deletions wait in the Trash
Links
The chat lists every note a run made or changed; Copy link puts its share link on the clipboard
Other models
OpenAI, Gemini, Aster, any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, each with your own key
Meetings
Summaries written by Claude when a key is present, on the device otherwise

Calendar and meetings

Google Calendar
Today's events at the head of home; a to-do with a due day is an all-day event within seconds; moves and goes with the to-do; only events Ghost.md made are changed
iCal
Any address (iCloud, Outlook, Fastmail); nothing to sign in to
Queries
from: events, shown as a list, table, month or timeline
Meetings
Ghost joins Google Meet and Zoom from its own account, invited as you@in.ghostmarkdown.com or by a pasted link; every occurrence of a series; live transcript with speakers; summary and to-dos; filed into the right workspace's Meetings folder; posted to Slack if chosen

Voice

Speak a note
Said, written as Markdown with to-dos as to-dos; "actually …" takes a line back
Record
A meeting, even with the phone locked; transcribed and summarised on the device; the tape stays with the note
Read aloud
ElevenLabs plugin, in a voice you choose; narrated onboarding and Academy
Where
Android and the Mac; the microphone is on only while you record

Files and sync

Layout
Library/ with .glyph/config.json (folders, settings), .trash/, image/ file/ video/ recordings/, Inbox/, workspaces/<name>/
Interop
Obsidian opens the folder; GitHub renders it; one-block front matter read everywhere; a ticket made in Obsidian works
Sync
Optional account; notes, tapes, pictures and settings encrypted on the device, end to end; a fresh device restores from the files
Backup
Back up to git: commits to GitHub or any host, pushes as you write, restores from a clone; or one .ghostex export, password protected if you like
Updates
In the app, from the same files this page links
