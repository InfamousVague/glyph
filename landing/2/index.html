Notes, journals, boards, canvases and tickets, kept as plain Markdown on your own devices and sealed when they sync. Claude is built in.

Download for freeOpen Ghost.md in your browser

Claude, in the app and at your notes.

Ask in the chat and the note opens as Claude starts writing, word by word at its own caret. Or add Ghost.md as a connector and let claude.ai, Claude Desktop and Claude Code read, search and write your notes, tickets and comments.

A whole team's work, in a notebook.

Give a notebook a key and its notes become tickets, with statuses, assignees, priorities and labels. Boards, a Gantt you can drag, cycles with a burndown, epics and a roadmap, all drawn from a query, all still Markdown.

Send Ghost to the meeting instead.

Invite it to a Google Meet or Zoom call and it joins by itself, writes the transcript as the call runs, and files a summary with who does what next, posted to Slack if you like.

Your calendar lives in your notes.

Today's events at the head of home. A to-do with a due day is on your Google Calendar seconds later; tick it off and it is gone. Paste any iCal address and those events are in too.

Switch on what you need. Nothing else.

Notion, Slack, GitHub, Todoist, Things, Lovart, ElevenLabs, webhooks and more are built in and off by default. Each is a switch in the Market, and Local only holds back every one that uses the internet.

Sealed. Yours. Readable anywhere.

No account needed. With one, notes, tapes and pictures are encrypted on the device, end to end, and a team's notes with a key only its members hold. Every one of them is a .md file you can open in Obsidian, on GitHub, or in git.
