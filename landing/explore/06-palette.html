Plain Markdown core

Every note is one .md file, named after its title, in its workspace's folder. Journals, boards, canvases, notebooks and tickets are Markdown too, so Obsidian opens them and GitHub renders them. Pictures, files and recordings live under Library/, the trash is Library/.trash/.

Speak a note android · mac

Say what you want written and it arrives as Markdown, its to-dos as to-dos. Record a meeting, even with the phone locked; afterwards the device transcribes it and writes a summary under the title. The microphone is on only while you record.

Journals core

Dated entries, any number a day, from your own template, listed by month.

Canvases core

Ideas on a page, joined by labelled lines, kept as a .canvas file Obsidian reads.

Version history core

A note keeps every version of itself in a file beside it: who, when, the lines out and the lines in, as git keeps a change. Restore any, and undo the restore.

v2 2026-09-29T16:20:05Z sam dfb138ca "Date picked"
  =3
  -- [ ] Pick a launch date ^pick-date
  +- [x] Pick a launch date ^pick-date

Marks plugin

A spoiler in smoke, highlights, asides, redactions and effects. A line starting = shows its answer. A mermaid fence is drawn. A YouTube, Spotify or Figma link alone on a line is a card.

Tickets teams

A note whose front matter says it is a ticket. Keys like HT-12, statuses, assignees, due days, priorities, labels, components, estimates, sub-tickets with a progress bar, a check before a blocked ticket is done, templates for new ones, duplicates shown as it is made, Merge into… with one Undo.

---
type: ticket
id: PRD-11
status: In progress
assignee: Sam
priority: highest
---

Queries teams

What matches across every note, as a table, a list, a board, a calendar, a timeline, a chart or a rollup. Totals add up as you go; pick a few cards and change them all at once.

```query
from: tickets [[Product]]
where: status != Done
show: board
```

Gantt teams

show: gantt draws a query's tickets as bars across their days. Drag a bar to move or stretch it, link what waits on what, mark a milestone; Week, Month and Quarter to zoom. Still Markdown.

Cycles, epics and a roadmap teams

Cycles with a burndown and velocity, and unfinished work carried to the next. Epics that add up their tickets, milestones, and a roadmap drawn from them.

My tickets and Triage teams

Everything assigned to you across every notebook, by when it is due. Triage works a queue by keys: J and K to move, A to assign, P for priority, T for done. Watch a ticket, read its history, get a morning digest.

Forms teams

Share a link and anyone can fill the form in, with no account. Each answer arrives sealed and becomes a ticket in the notebook, ready to triage.

Rules teams

Sentences in a rules fence in the notebook, run as its tickets change. A GitHub pull request sits on its ticket; Start and Stop keep the time spent.

```rules
When status becomes Done, post to Slack #launches.
```

Organizations teams

Invite people by handle or link. Shared notebooks and folders, sealed with a key only members hold. Live cursors in each member's colour, @mention a teammate or a day, verify a page as the team's current truth, comments in the margin, an audit log.

Meetings plugin

Invite you@in.ghostmarkdown.com to a Google Meet or Zoom call, or paste a link. Ghost joins by itself, every occurrence of a series, writes the transcript as the call runs with who said what, and files a summary with the to-dos as to-dos, posted to Slack if you like.

Calendar plugin

Today's events at the head of home. A to-do with a due day is an all-day event on Google Calendar a few seconds later. Paste any iCal address and its events are in too. Only the events Ghost.md made are ever changed.

Claude in the app plugin

Ask in the chat with your own Anthropic key and the note opens as Claude starts writing, word by word at its own caret. Its thinking streams in, with a line for every note it reads. Talk to it from the dock. The key stays in the device's secure storage.

Claude connector mcp

Add Ghost.md as a connector and claude.ai, Claude Desktop and Claude Code read, search and write your notes, tickets and comments. Claude shows as online while it works, signs what it writes, and never writes over an edit from another device.

claude mcp add --transport http glyph https://ghostmarkdown.com/api/mcp

Notion plugin

Sign in with Notion and pick a board. A to-do in any note can become a task there, one at a time or a whole list, in step both ways. Open it in a drawer without leaving the note.

GitHub, and Back up to git plugins

A note linked to a repo sends its items as issues, ticked both ways; a pull request sits on its ticket. Back up to git commits your whole library to GitHub or any host, pushes as you write, restores a fresh device from a clone, and can publish it as a site.

Slack, Discord and webhooks plugins

Post a note, a meeting's summary or the team's news to a channel. Webhooks out tell a URL when notes change; webhooks in add to a note from Zapier, Make, IFTTT or curl.

The Market site

Plugins, themes, fonts and icons, all built in: adding one is a switch, nothing to download. Todoist, Things, Lovart, ElevenLabs, OpenAI, Gemini, Contacts, place reminders, keyboard modes and more. Browse it here and open any of it in the app.

Sealed sync core

No account needed; without one, nothing of yours is on a server. With one, notes, tapes and pictures are encrypted on the device, end to end. No ads, no analytics. Local only holds back every plugin that uses the internet.

Take it all with you core

Export everything as one .ghostex file, password protected if you like. Or copy the folder: it already is the export.
