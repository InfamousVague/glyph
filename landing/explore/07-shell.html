Ghost.md / Claude.md

Claude, built in and connected.

Ask Claude inside Ghost.md and watch it write into your notes as it thinks. Or let Claude's own apps read and write them, and see it working, live.

  • Watch it type. The note opens as Claude starts writing; its words arrive at its own caret, and the page follows them.
  • Its thinking, shown. What it is weighing streams in, with a line for every note it reads or changes.
  • Your key, your device. Kept in secure storage; what you ask goes to Anthropic and nowhere else.
  • From claude.ai, Claude Desktop and Claude Code. Add the connector and Claude reads, searches and writes notes, tickets and comments, shown as online while it works.
claude mcp add --transport http glyph https://ghostmarkdown.com/api/mcp

Ghost.md / For teams.md

Tickets, cycles and a Gantt, in files the team owns.

Give a notebook a key and its notes become tickets. Boards, a Gantt, burndowns and a roadmap are drawn from a query, and all of it stays Markdown.

  • Tickets in notebooks. Keys, statuses, assignees, due days, priorities, labels, sub-tickets, templates.
  • Boards, tables, calendars, charts. One query fence, shown any way; bulk edits with one Undo.
  • Cycles, epics, a roadmap. Burndown and velocity, work carried over, epics that add up.
  • My tickets and Triage. Everything assigned to you; Triage by keys.
  • Forms. Share a link; each answer lands sealed as a ticket.
  • Rules, PRs, timers, watchers, history, digest, merge. "When status becomes Done, post to Slack."

Organizations

Shared notebooks and folders, sealed with a key only members hold. Live cursors in each member's colour, @mentions, verified pages, comments in the margin, an audit log.

Ghost.md / Calendar.md

Your calendar, in your notes.

Connect Google Calendar and your day is one page: today's events at the head of home, above your notes, and your dated to-dos on your calendar.

  • To-dos on your calendar, within seconds. Change the day and it moves; tick it off and it is gone.
  • Events you can query. As a list, a table, a month or a timeline.
  • Any calendar. Paste an iCal address; nothing to sign in to.
```query
from: events
where: start >= today
show: calendar
```

Ghost.md / Meetings.md

Ghost goes to your meetings.

Invite Ghost to a Google Meet or Zoom call and it joins by itself, writes the transcript as the call runs, and files it as a note: who said what, and a summary of what was decided and who does what next.

Invite
you@in.ghostmarkdown.com on the calendar invite, or paste a link and press Join
Series
every occurrence; skip one or stop from Settings › Meetings
Written up
on your device, to-dos as to-dos, filed into the right workspace's Meetings folder
Slack
the summary posted to a channel, if you choose

Ghost.md / Versions.md

Every version, kept like git.

A note can keep every version of itself, in a file beside it. Open the history and it is a timeline of who changed what, and when. Restore any, and undo the restore.

# Ghost.md versions 1
v1 2026-09-26T11:03:47.000Z matt 0351c39d full
  +# Launch plan
  +- [ ] Ship the pricing page ^ship-page
  +- [ ] Pick a launch date ^pick-date
v2 2026-09-29T16:20:05.000Z sam dfb138ca "Date picked"
  =2
  -- [ ] Pick a launch date ^pick-date
  +- [x] Pick a launch date ^pick-date

Each version is who and when, then the lines out and in, as git keeps a change. Every fiftieth is a whole copy, and each is checked against its hash. Two devices that both kept versions are merged, so none is lost.
