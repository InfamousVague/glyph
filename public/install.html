Install →

 

  1. Tap Install. Chrome downloads the app.
  2. Open the download. If Android asks, allow installs from Chrome and go back.
  3. Tap Update or Install. Your notes stay where they are.
  4. From here Glyph updates itself: new versions arrive while it is open and apply the next time it starts. When a change needs a new app, Glyph offers it in the app.

Not on your phone? Open the web version.
