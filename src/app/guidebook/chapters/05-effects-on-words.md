# Effects on words

_Five marks that move: heat, frost, a wave, a shimmer and a haunting, each written as its own emoji twice on either side._

The effects come from the Marks plugin, beside Ghost.md's six still marks ([[The marks you can type]]). Where those change how words look, these make them move. They are for reading, not hiding: the words stay where they are, and the line never shifts as an effect comes or goes.

## The five

| Effect | Type | What it does | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heat | `🔥🔥too hot to touch🔥🔥` | The words go bold, with the faintest waver of their own, and the text on the line above them wavers as if seen through the hot air coming off them. The line above that wavers too, more gently. | "heated … end heated" |
| Frost | `❄️❄️frozen solid❄️❄️` | The words go cold, and a grainy rime creeps over their edges, holds, and draws back again, every seven seconds: pale ice on a dark page, deeper ice on a light one. | "frosted … end frosted" |
| Wave | `🌊🌊out to sea🌊🌊` | The words bob along the line, a ripple passing through them from the first letter to the last. | "wavy … end wavy" |
| Shimmer | `✨✨silver thread✨✨` | Every few seconds a glint slides across the words: a glow on a dark page, a pale sheen on a light one. | "shimmering … end shimmering" |
| Haunt | `👻👻nobody there👻👻` | The words fade almost away and back, slowly, the fading drifting along them like something passing. | "haunted … end haunted" |

Here is each at work. The heat is on the second line, so the first has something to waver:

The air over the stove, in August.
🔥🔥too hot to touch🔥🔥

❄️❄️frozen solid❄️❄️, 🌊🌊out to sea🌊🌊, ✨✨silver thread✨✨ and 👻👻nobody there👻👻.

Heat's haze reaches over one blank line, since most paragraphs have one between them. The gentler second line stops at a blank. With no text above them, heated words are bold and barely moving.

## How they behave

- **Two on each side.** One emoji is just an emoji, and three in a row are three emoji. A flame in a sentence stays a flame.
- **They nest.** `🔥🔥a hot ✨✨glinting✨✨ one🔥🔥` heats the whole stretch and makes one word glint as well.
- **They lift for editing.** Put the caret in the words and the effect stops, so you edit plain text; move the caret away and it starts again. A note that cannot be edited never lifts them.
- **They hold still when asked.** When the phone or the Mac is set to reduce motion, each effect is drawn and holds still: the heat haze and the frost at rest, the haunting half there, the wave and the shimmer plain. Settings › Animations does not change them. Its Animation speed and its three switches are for the app's other movement, and the device's own setting comes first.
- **They are on the Style page and the cheat sheet** with the other marks: press and hold on words, choose Style, and each effect has a word of its own.

## Saying them

While recording, say the effect as an adjective on each side of the words: "the stove is heated too hot to touch end heated". The adjectives are heated, frosted, wavy, shimmering and haunted.

They are adjectives because the nouns are everyday words. A spoken mark closes on "and" as well as "end", since speech recognition often hears one for the other, so "heat the oven and heat the pan" would have heated "the oven". "Heated … and heated" is rare enough in speech to be safe. Both halves are needed: "a heated debate" on its own stays words. More on this in [[Saying the marks]].

## Anywhere else

In another app the emoji stay, so `🔥🔥too hot to touch🔥🔥` still says what was meant. Switch the Marks plugin off, in Settings › Plugins, and Ghost.md shows them as plain characters too, as it does the other marks.
