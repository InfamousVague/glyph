# The first five minutes

_What Ghost.md shows you when it first opens, the notes it can hand you to learn from, and where to go next._

## The welcome guide

The first time Ghost.md opens, the welcome guide comes up over it. If the side key opened it, the welcome guide waits for the next launch, since you are already talking. It has six pages, and a row of dots at the top says which one you are on. Next appears once you reach the foot of a page. Until then, after a moment, a button with an arrow offers to take you down. Back steps back, and so do the phone's back gesture and a swipe to the right. Skip closes it from any page, and on the last page it reads Close.

| Page | What it says | What it asks |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome to Ghost.md | Notes you type or say, in plain Markdown, kept on your own devices and the same on every one. | Nothing. |
| Light or dark? | The page changes the moment you tap, so you choose by looking. | Dark, Light, or Match the phone. |
| Choose your model | The model that rewrites your notes runs on the phone. Bigger is more careful, and slower. | One of four models, each with its size. |
| Make the side key record | On Android, how to make Ghost.md the phone's digital assistant, row by row for your phone, so that holding the side key starts a note. | Nothing to pick here. Open assistant settings takes you to the right screen, and the page checks when you come back. |
| Every mark, side by side | What you type on the left, how the note draws it on the right. | Nothing. |
| A few habits | How to talk so the cues are heard: pause before a cue word, or say it on its own; stop for two seconds to start a paragraph. | Nothing. The last button, Try it, starts a voice note. |

Anywhere but Android, the fourth page says instead: "Start a voice note with Speak."

The chosen model is not downloaded yet. Tap "get it now" under the list to fetch it, or fetch it later with Get beside it in Settings › Formatting › Model. Until then, asking the AI for something on a note only says "The AI needs a model on the phone." The page says the model downloads the first time you ask; it does not ([[The models on your phone]]).

Hold the side key before you reach its page and the welcome guide comes back with one line at the top: "Not yet, finish reading." Nothing records. From the side-key page on, a held key records as it always does.

Every choice can be changed later: the theme in Settings › Appearance › Page, the model in Settings › Formatting. The whole welcome guide opens again from Settings › About › How to talk to Ghost.md, starting at its first page.

## The notes it gives you

A library with nothing in it gets one note the first time it opens: [[How to format a note]]. It is a short tutorial, one mark at a time: how to type it, how to say it, and an example, with a photograph of smoke to show a picture. Try each one, then delete it. A library that already has notes never gets it, so an update never drops a note on you.

Settings › About has a row for it, and four more:

- **Add Ghost.md: The Guide** adds this book, 44 chapters and its index, and opens the index. Pressed again, it opens the book you have. Its chapters are then your newest notes, so the first six fill Recent until you write something else.
- **Add the sample note** makes another [[How to format a note]], every mark in it.
- **Add the example board** makes [[Launch week]]: a working board written in Markdown, with columns, cards and the list items they stand for.
- **Add the example canvas** makes [[Cabin weekend, laid out]]: cards on a page with lines between them, one of everything a canvas can hold.
- **Add the “How Ghost.md works” canvas** makes [[How Ghost.md works]]: eight cards, in order, from saying a note to where it can go. Two of them stand for the example board and the sample note.

Each of the other four makes a new note and opens it, and makes another copy on every press. The links above open these notes once they are in your library. Before then they are drawn dashed, and a tap on one makes a new, empty note with that name as its heading, which is what a link to a missing note does. So add the example board and the two canvases from About before you follow their links in this book. Added after a tap has made the empty note, the example board leaves you two notes called Launch week.

## Your first note

Tap **+** and choose **Note**. A blank page opens. Write a first line: that line is the note's name, in your list and on its file. Put `#` and a space in front of it to make it a title. There is nothing to save: the note is kept as you type.

The **+** sheet also offers a Canvas, a Book, and From a shared link. Each has a chapter of its own.

## Your first spoken note

Tap **Speak** on the home page and talk. Until the first words arrive, the ghost listens and a card suggests things to say. Then your words appear on the page as they are understood, with the spoken cues turning into marks. Tap **Done** to keep the note, or **Discard**.

In the app, while Review after recording is on, as it is to begin with, Done opens the new note and the review looks it over ([[Spoken asks and the review]]). On Android it can be switched off in Settings › Recording. With it off, over a locked phone, or in a browser tab, you come back to the home page instead, with the new note at the top of Recent.

On Android, once the side key is set up, hold it, let go, and talk. It works from the lock screen. Hold the key again to stop, which saves the note, the same as Done.

The first time the app opens it fetches its voice model, about 60 MB. While it comes down, the home page says so, with how much has come: "Downloading the voice model, … Keep Ghost.md open." If it fails, the line says so and offers Try again. In a browser tab there is no model to fetch: the browser's own speech recognition, where it has one, hears you instead ([[What stays on your phone]]).

## Learning the marks

**Ghost.md Academy** teaches Markdown one mark at a time. Open it from Settings › About › Ghost.md Academy, or from its card on the home page, which offers itself until you pass a lesson or put it away with its cross.

There are thirteen lessons: a title, a heading, bold, italic, struck through, code, a link, a list, steps in order, a to-do, a quote, a dividing line, and a block of code. Each shows one line using its mark. Then you write your own in a field, and underneath it the note's own editor draws what you typed, changing as you type. The lesson is ticked the moment the mark appears, and Next moves on. **Show me** writes the example in for you, **Show me a hint** says how, and **Skip** passes over it. Nothing you type there becomes a note.

Progress is kept on this device, so the Academy opens at the first lesson you have not passed. At the end, any lesson can be taken again, Start again clears the ticks, and Cheat sheet opens the rest.

The **cheat sheet** is every mark on one page: Settings › Cheat sheet, or Help › Formatting cheat sheet in any note's More sheet (the three dots). The marks come in groups: Words, Lines, Pointing somewhere, Raised and lowered, Blocks, and Ghost.md's own. Each is a card with its characters, the line to type, and that line as the note draws it. The field at the top finds a mark by its name or its characters: "bold", `||` or "board".
