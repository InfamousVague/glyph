  3. Go through the welcome

    Required

    A short walkthrough opens the first time. A voice reads each page; switch it off at the top if you're somewhere quiet.

    • On the texts page, tap Use beside Google Messages. Your texts stay where they are.
    • Don't make Ghost your default texting app for now. You can always switch later.
    • On the texts page, tap Set as default SMS app and say yes when Android asks.
    • If Android refuses, tap Open Android's page for Ghost Messaging, then ⋮ › Allow restricted settings, and try again.
    Turn off RCS chats first. While they're on in Google Messages, people who use RCS keep sending you chats that never arrive as texts. The welcome shows a Coming from Google Messages? Do this first card that opens the right setting.
