Scribe

Turn a recorded call into words, on your device.

Scribe turns a recorded call into words: every line with who said it and when, made on your device. Because Record call keeps each voice on its own track, Scribe always knows who's speaking.

Add it: Settings › Plugins › Scribe › Add, or for a whole circle. Needs Android 13 or later, or a Mac.

Write out a recording

  1. Open Recordings

    From a call's menu, or wherever you keep recordings, tap one, then Write out the words.

  2. Read, copy or send

    When it's done, Read the words shows each line, named and timed. Copy them, or send them to the space.

Choices

  • Where your phone has its own summary model, a line sums the call up at the top.
  • The words show as subtitles over the recording in the player.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere: the words are made on your device.
