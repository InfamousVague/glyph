Messaging

Texts and conversations

Sending, reactions, edits, ticks, stickers, effects and conversation settings.

Your texts (SMS and picture messages) work as in any texting app, with more: reactions, edits, effects, stickers and voice messages.

The conversations list

  • Swipe a conversation left to archive it; keep swiping to delete. Swipe right to pin it to the top.
  • Long-press one for pin, mute, block, delete and more, or to choose several.
  • Search at the top finds names, numbers and words.
  • Mark all read (the round button by the title) covers spaces, Telegram and Signal too.

Sending

Type and send as usual. Beside the box: pictures (up to five in one message), stickers, GIFs and emoji, and the microphone for a voice message (press and hold where Send would be).

Ticks

MarkMeans
A clockSending.
One tickSent.
Two ticksDelivered to their phone. In colour: read (picture and group messages, from phones that say so).
A lockEncrypted: see Encrypted texts.

Delivery reports and read receipts are in Settings › Receipts.

Press and hold a message

  • React with an emoji; it shows under the message.
  • Edit: a text can't change once it's on their phone, so you choose Change on this phone only or Send correction.
  • Effects: send it with a screen effect (confetti, hearts and 18 more), seen by anyone using Ghost.

A conversation's settings

Tap the cog, or the conversation's name at the top:

A group text also has Group name, Add people and Leave group. A chat background syncs to the others if they use Ghost too.

Looks, stickers and effects

Settings › Appearance has the looks (Ghost, Mist, Lamplight, Sharp, Phosphor and Dial-Up), light and dark, your bubble colour and text size. Settings › Emoji, Settings › Effects and Settings › Sounds have the rest.
