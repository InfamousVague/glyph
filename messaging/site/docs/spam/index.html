Spam filter

Texts that look like spam, kept out of your way.

The spam filter moves texts that look like spam, from numbers not in your contacts, to a Spam tab. Nothing is deleted or blocked: a conversation in Spam opens like any other.

On from the start. Its choices are in Settings › Spam filter.

Spam, and not spam

  • Open the Spam tab to see what was moved.
  • Not spam on a conversation puts it back in Messages for good.
  • Move to Spam on any conversation sends it there.

How strict, and your own lists

  • Signs to look for: switch off a sign that catches texts you want.
  • Go easy on sign-in codes and short codes: real codes and company numbers need one sign more.
  • Where it looks: group chats with strangers, and Signal and Telegram chats from people you haven't added.

It all happens on your phone; texts are sent nowhere.
