Live captions

See what everyone in a call says, as they say it.

Live captions shows what everyone in a call says, written out as they talk, each line named. Each person's words are written out on their own device and shared with the call, so nobody's voice goes to a server.

Add it: Settings › Plugins › Live captions › Add, or for a whole circle.

Turn captions on

In a call, open the call's menu and tap Show captions. Hide captions turns them off again. It's for you alone; others choose for themselves.

In your language

With Translate added too, captions in another language are translated into yours, on your device.

In the alpha

Captions across several real devices are still being tried. Tell us how they do in the Alpha testers circle.
