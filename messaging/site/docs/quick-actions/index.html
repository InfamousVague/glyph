Quick actions

Dates, addresses, tracking numbers and phone numbers become buttons.

Quick actions puts a button under a message for things in it: a date, an address, a tracking number or a phone number.

On from the start.

ButtonFor
Add to calendarA day or time: "Friday at 7", "tomorrow 9:30am", "Nov 20". The calendar opens with it filled in.
Open in MapsA street address.
TrackA UPS, USPS or FedEx tracking number: the carrier's page for it.
Call and CopyA phone number or a code.

Found in the message's words, on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.
