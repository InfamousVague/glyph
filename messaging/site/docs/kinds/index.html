Kinds of text

Texts from strangers sorted by what they are about.

Kinds of text sorts texts from numbers not in your contacts by what they're about: deliveries and tracking, food delivery, appointments, payment reminders, notifications and receipts, sales and solicitors, political texts, debt collectors and likely scams. A filter beside the tabs shows or hides each kind in Messages.

On from the start. Its choices are in Settings › Kinds of text.

The filter

Tap the filter beside the tabs. Tick the kinds to show in Messages; some, like political texts and debt collectors, start hidden. All at the top ticks or unticks every kind.

"What the filter hides" is said at the foot of the list, with a way to see it: nothing goes missing quietly. Not … on a conversation puts it back in Messages for good.

Choices

  • How strict: how many signs it takes to sort a text.
  • Kinds: switch off a kind to stop sorting texts into it.
  • Your own words: words that sort a stranger's text into a kind.
  • Spam before shown kinds: a fake "your parcel is held, pay the fee" stays in Spam, not Deliveries.

It happens on your phone. Your contacts are never sorted, and nothing is deleted or blocked.
