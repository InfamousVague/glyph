Circles, spaces and calls

Spaces

The four kinds of space, polls, mentions, and spaces of your own.

Spaces are the rooms of a circle. Each has a chat, and what else it does depends on its kind.

The four kinds

KindForMore
Chat and voiceA chat, and a call anyone can hop in and out of, like a voice channel.Calls
TextingA chat on its own.
FilesFolders of files, sealed end to end, with a chat beside them.Files spaces
SMS spaceA chat that people without the app are in by text, from one member's phone.SMS spaces

To add one: New space on the circle's dock (if your role may manage spaces). To rename, move, change or delete spaces: Circle settings › Spaces › Arrange spaces. Spaces with the same heading are listed together.

The chat

Every space's chat takes words, pictures, files, GIFs, stickers and voice messages. Long-press a message for reactions, reply, copy and delete.

Mentions

Type @ at the start of a word and pick someone from the list. They get a notification, the message stands out for them, and unread marks show @ when something unread mentions you. Turn mention notifications off in Settings › Notifications.

Polls

  1. Ask one

    In a space's chat, tap +, then Poll. Write a question and two to ten answers, and choose whether people may pick one or several.

  2. Vote

    The poll is a card with each answer's share. Tap an answer to vote; tap it again to take your vote back. Late arrivals see the votes so far.

Spaces of your own

A space doesn't have to be in a circle: New › New space makes one you can share with friends. Every group of friends comes with its own space too. Spaces of your own show in Messages with their newest message, and swipe left to archive like a conversation.
