Computers

Mac and Windows apps

Ghost Messaging on a computer, with or without your phone.

Ghost Messaging runs on a Mac and on Windows as well as your phone. On its own, a computer has your friends, circles, calls, files and your Telegram and Signal chats. Paired with your Android phone, it has your texts too.

Install

  • Mac: download the disk image from gogho.st, open it, drag Ghost Messaging to Applications. Signed and checked by Apple.
  • Windows: download and run ghost-messaging-setup.exe. Signed.

Both update themselves. See Install the app.

The first time

  • Sign in to your Ghost account to be the same you as on your phone: same friends, same circles. See Your account.
  • Link an Android phone to see and send your texts: see Pair a computer with your phone. It's optional.

Keyboard

  • Enter sends; Shift+Enter starts a new line.
  • Esc closes a picture or menu.
  • Drag files onto a Files space, or a conversation, to send them.
