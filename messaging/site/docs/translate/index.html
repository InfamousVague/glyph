Translate

Read texts in other languages, translated on your phone.

Translate reads texts in other languages for you: the translation is in the bubble, with the words as they were sent in a dimmed card above. It all happens on your phone; nothing is sent to a translation service.

Add it: Settings › Plugins › Translate › Add. It starts off because it downloads language models.

What you see

  • Tap the dimmed card to swap the translation and the original.
  • A message's menu has Translate and Copy translation.
  • It works on texts, Telegram, Signal and space chats. On a paired computer, your phone does the translating.
  • Only texts that came to you are translated, never yours, and only ones with enough words to tell their language.
