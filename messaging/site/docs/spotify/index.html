Spotify

A queue of songs for a circle's call, played from one person's Spotify.

Spotify plays music to a circle's call. One person's own Spotify app plays, and everyone in the call hears it in stereo and can add songs to one queue.

Turn it on: for a whole circle, in Circle settings › Plugins › Spotify. It does nothing outside a circle's spaces.

Start the music

Someone in the call on a Mac or a PC with Spotify can start it. On a phone, in a browser or on Linux you can listen and queue songs.

  1. Open Spotify

    Open the Spotify app on your Mac or PC and sign in. Choosing which song plays needs Spotify Premium.

  2. Join the call and press Start

    Tap the note in the space's header, or Spotify in the call's menu, then Start. What you were already playing is what the call hears first.

  3. On a Mac, say yes twice

    The first time, macOS asks whether Ghost Messaging may control Spotify, and whether it may record Spotify's sound. If you said no, turn it on in System Settings › Privacy & Security, under Automation and Screen & System Audio Recording. Windows asks nothing.

One person plays at a time: the first, until they press Stop or leave. Then someone else can start. Stopping pauses a song from the queue, and leaves one you chose in Spotify playing for you.

Queue songs

In Spotify, press Share › Copy Song Link, and paste it under Add a song. Anyone whose role may speak in the space can add songs, move them, pause and skip.

  • Links to albums, playlists and podcasts aren't songs: paste a song's link.
  • You can take away a song you added. Whoever plays the music can take away any.
  • The queue holds 100 songs, 20 from each person. It is kept on the computer that plays, and goes when the music stops.
  • If whoever plays picks a song in Spotify themselves, that plays, and the queue goes on after it. When the queue runs out, the music pauses.

While music plays, Spotify is listed among the people in the call, and a line over the call has the song, pause, skip and Queue.

What you hear

The music has a loudness of its own on your device, under On this device; at 0% it isn't sent to you at all. Deafened, you hear none of it. It is never in a recording of the call, its captions or Scribe. Whoever plays it hears it from Spotify itself.

Wear headphones if you play it

On speakers, your microphone hears the music and sends it a second time.

On a PC

Windows opens each song in Spotify, and Spotify sometimes shows a song without starting it. Ghost Messaging asks it to play; if it still doesn't, you are told to press play there. It needs Windows 10 version 2004 or later. A Mac needs macOS 14.4 or later.

What it uses

  • The DJ's own Spotify, live. Ghost Messaging presses Spotify's own buttons and sends the sound of the Spotify app, and of no other app, from that computer to each person in the call, encrypted as the call is. It is never stored, there is no Spotify password or key, and nothing is kept on the relay.
  • Spotify's terms. They are for personal use and don't allow rebroadcasting, so this is for a small private call, and the account of whoever plays carries that risk.
  • Titles and covers. The device that adds a song asks Spotify what it is called, and each device in the call loads its cover from Spotify.
