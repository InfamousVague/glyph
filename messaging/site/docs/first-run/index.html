Getting started

The first time you open it

The welcome, texting with Ghost or beside Google Messages, and picking a look.

The first time you open Ghost Messaging, a short welcome walks you through it. A voice reads each page; switch it off with Voice off at the top if you're somewhere quiet. You can watch it again any time from Settings › About.

The six pages

  1. Everyone, in one place: what Ghost is.
  2. Start with your texts: whether Ghost takes over your texts (below).
  3. Texts and chats, side by side: friends, circles and your other chats.
  4. Drop in, out loud: voice spaces you can hop into.
  5. On every screen: the Mac and Windows apps.
  6. You're in: pick a look, light or dark, and your bubble colour.

Texting with Ghost, or beside Google Messages

On an Android phone, the texts page asks how Ghost should handle your texts. Either way, nothing is deleted: your texts stay in Android's own store.

ChoiceWhat happensGood for
Set as default SMS appGhost sends and receives your texts and picture messages. Android asks you to confirm.Making Ghost your one texting app.
Use beside Google MessagesGoogle Messages stays your texting app; Ghost reads your texts and its notifications and shows them alongside everything else.Trying Ghost first, or keeping RCS chats. See Beside Google Messages.

Change your mind any time: Settings › Messages, or Android's Settings › Apps › Default apps.

Coming from Google Messages or Samsung Messages

If you make Ghost your texting app, a card says Coming from Google Messages? Do this first. Do it: while RCS chats are on in your old app, people who use RCS keep sending you chats that never arrive as texts, and look like they've gone missing.

In Google Messages, that's your profile picture, then Messages settings › RCS chats, off.

What it asks for, and why

AskedWhy
Texts (SMS and picture messages)To show, send and receive them.
ContactsFor names and pictures, and to find which of your contacts use Ghost. Read on the phone only.
NotificationsTo tell you when a message comes.
Microphone and cameraOnly when you record a voice message, take a picture, or join a call.
