Getting started

Welcome

What Ghost Messaging is, and where to start.

Ghost Messaging is one app for the people you talk to: your texts, your friends on Ghost, your circles with their chats, calls and files, and your Telegram, Signal and other chats, side by side. Messages between Ghost apps are sealed end to end, there are no ads, and the app is open source.

Ghost Messaging is in alpha

It works well enough to use every day, but things still change quickly and some features are marked Coming soon. If you're testing it, start with Join the alpha, and tell us anything odd in the Alpha testers circle.

Finding your way around

Everything starts from the dock at the foot of the screen. From left to right: New (start a conversation, a circle or a space, or join one), Messages, Contacts, Archive and Settings.

At the top of Messages are three tabs: Messages (texts and chats), Codes (sign-in codes, kept out of the way) and Circles.

A few words we use

WordWhat it means
CircleA group of people with a sidebar of spaces, a bit like a small server. You can be in as many as you like.
SpaceA room in a circle (or on its own): chat and voice, text only, files, or an SMS space for people without the app.
FriendSomeone you talk to over Ghost rather than by text: added by their username, or by their number once you've sealed your texts together.
ConnectionsYour other chat accounts linked into Ghost: Telegram, Signal, WhatsApp, Matrix.
RoutesHow messages travel between Ghost apps: over the relay, or by text.
PluginAn extra you add from Settings › Plugins: for yourself, or for everyone in a circle.

On which devices

  • Android phone: everything, including your texts (SMS and picture messages).
  • Mac and Windows: friends, circles, calls, files and your other chats on their own, and your texts when paired with your Android phone.
  • iPhone Coming soon: friends, circles and calls. Apple doesn't let apps send or read texts outside the EU, so no texts.
