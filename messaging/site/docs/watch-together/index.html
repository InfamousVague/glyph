Watch together

Play a video or song for everyone in a call, in step.

Watch together plays a video or a song from a Files space on everyone's device in a call, in step. Pause it, and it pauses for everyone.

Add it: Settings › Plugins › Watch together › Add, or for a whole circle.

Start watching

  1. Be in a call

    Join the call of a chat-and-voice space first.

  2. Pick a file

    Open a Files space in the same circle, press and hold a video or song, and tap Watch together in the call.

  3. Everyone sees it

    A line over the call says who's watching what: tap Watch to join in. Stop ends it. Play, pause and moving through it happen for everyone.

Everyone in the call needs to be able to open the Files space the file is in.
