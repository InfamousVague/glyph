Messaging

Encrypted texts

Seal texts with one person end to end, and check it is really them.

Texts normally travel through your carrier as plain words. With someone who also has Ghost, you can seal your texts end to end, so only your two phones can read them, whatever carries them.

Turn it on with someone

  1. Open the conversation's Encryption

    In your conversation with them, tap the cog (or their name), then Encryption.

  2. Send my key

    Encrypt your texts with them so only your two phones can read them. They need Ghost Messaging too. This sends them one text with your key.

  3. They turn it on

    They see your key arrive and tap Turn on. From then on your texts with them are sealed, and show a lock.

Ghost can say who has it

Ghost sends an invisible hello to find out who else uses it, so it can offer encryption. Switch that off in Settings › Encryption if you'd rather not.

Check it's really them

Compare the safety number, 30 digits shown on both phones, side by side or on a call. If they match, tap They match: mark as verified. If they ever change, Ghost tells you: their key changed, perhaps a new phone, perhaps someone else.

What it covers, and what it doesn't

SealedNot sealed
Your texts with that one personGroup texts
Pictures and voice messages sent as picture messages (MMS)
Who you text and when: your carrier still sees that

Messages between friends and in circles and spaces go over Ghost instead of texts, and are always sealed end to end, pictures and all.

Turning it off

In the conversation's Encryption, tap Turn off encryption. If their texts start coming unsealed (they removed Ghost, say), Ghost warns you and offers to turn it off.
