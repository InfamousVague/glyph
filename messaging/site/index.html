Ghost Messaging

A texting app for Android. Your SMS and picture messages, with reactions, effects and a choice of emoji, in the same quiet design as Ghost.md and Ghost Keyboard.

Download for Android
  • Replaces your texting appEvery conversation you already have is there the moment you switch, and still there if you switch back.
  • Reactions that landA ❤️ from an iPhone or a Pixel sits on the message it was for, not in a line of its own. Send yours back.
  • EffectsBalloons for "Happy birthday", confetti for "Congrats", lasers for "pew pew", and ghosts for "Boo".
  • Your emojiThe phone's own, Noto, Twemoji, OpenMoji or a clean outline set.
  • TicksSent, delivered, and read where the other phone says so.
  • Private by defaultNo account, no server of ours in the middle. The app connects to this site for its own updates and nothing else.

Installing

  1. Download the APK on your Android phone and open it. Allow your browser to install apps if Android asks.
  2. Open Ghost Messaging and choose "Set as default SMS app".
  3. That's it. It keeps itself up to date from here.
Texts and picture messages go through your carrier as they always have. RCS and iMessage are not available to apps other than Google's and Apple's own.
