On your computer

Everything, on the big screen.

Ghost Messaging for Mac and Windows shows every conversation, sends texts through your phone (sealed between the two), and joins calls and hangouts itself.

  1. Download for Mac or Windows. On a Mac, open the zip and drag it to Applications. On Windows, run the installer (Windows asks once, as it does for any new app).
  2. Pair with your phone. On your phone open Settings › Computers and scan the code the computer shows.
  3. That's it. Both keep themselves up to date.
Private by default

Nothing goes where it shouldn't.

No account to make. What travels through our server between friends is sealed on your device first, so the server passes it along and can't read it.

Sealed end to end

Chats between Ghost users, your computer's link to your phone, and calls are encrypted on the devices themselves.

No account

Your number stays on your phone. A username and linked accounts are yours to turn on, and to remove.

Texts stay texts

SMS and picture messages go through your carrier as they always have. Nothing is copied to us.
