On your computer

Your texts, on the big screen.

Ghost Messaging for Mac and Windows shows your phone's conversations and sends through your phone, sealed between the two. Calls and hangouts work there too.

  1. Download for Mac or Windows. On a Mac, open the zip and drag it to Applications. On Windows, run the installer (Windows asks once, as it does for any new app).
  2. Pair with your phone. On your phone open Settings › Computers and scan the code the computer shows.
  3. That's it. Both keep themselves up to date.
Private by default

Nothing goes anywhere it shouldn't.

No account to make. What travels through our server between friends is sealed on your phone first, so the server passes it along and can't read it.

Sealed end to end

Messages between Ghost users, your computer's link to your phone, and calls are encrypted on the devices themselves.

No account

Your number stays on your phone. A username and linked accounts are yours to turn on, and to remove.

Texts stay texts

SMS and picture messages go through your carrier as they always have. Nothing is copied to us.
