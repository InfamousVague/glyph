A chat-and-voice space has a call that's always open, like a voice channel: hop in, talk, hop out. Sound goes straight between the people in it, sealed end to end, at high quality.

Join a call

Open a chat-and-voice space and tap Join voice. Who's already in it shows above. On a phone, a space with people talking opens straight on its call.

Share your screen

Press Share screen in the call. In some call designs it's in the call's menu instead. It works on Mac, Windows, Linux and Android.

  • On a computer, pick a whole screen or one window. On a Mac, the first time, allow Ghost Messaging in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen Recording, then try again.
  • On Android, the phone asks before it shares, and you can choose one app or the whole screen. A notification says Sharing your screen while it's on.
Maya Chen's screenBack to call

Everyone else sees your screen large and whole, never cropped. Back to call puts it away, and a line over the call says who is sharing, with View to bring it back. While you share, a line says You're sharing your screen, with Stop.

Your screen goes out in place of your camera: while you share, others see your screen, not your camera, and your camera is back when you stop. Sound from your computer isn't shared yet. If your role in a space can't show video, it can't share a screen either. Like the rest of the call, it goes encrypted between you: the relay can't see it.

Sound settings

Settings › Voice and video has your microphone and speaker, the sound's quality, stereo, music mode (for playing an instrument), and noise removal (RNNoise or DeepFilterNet), which cleans background noise off your voice before it's sent. The call's menu has Voice settings… too.

Guests from a browser

Someone without the app can join a space's call from a browser. Copy the space's link from its menu (Invite people) and send it. They open it, choose their microphone and camera, and tap Join as a guest, or sign up with a username kept in that browser.

Calls connect through Ghost's relay first, which gets through almost any network: a phone on a mobile network, a workplace, school or hotel network. If the relay can't be reached, the call tries a direct path between you instead, by itself. The relay passes the call along still encrypted between you: it can't hear or see it.

With plugins

Add Record call, Live captions, Watch together, Soundboard or Auto leveler for more in calls. They show in the call's menu, and Record call and Soundboard put a button in the space's header.
