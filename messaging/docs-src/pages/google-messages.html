If you leave RCS chats on in Google Messages, people who use RCS keep sending you chats there that Ghost can't see. Turn it off in Google Messages: your profile picture, Messages settings › RCS chats.

Set up RCS beside Google Messages

Go to Settings › Messages › Set up RCS. Ghost checks each step and ticks it when it's done.

Ghost uses it for RCS. It's free from the Play Store: Get Google Messages if it isn't there.

In Android's Settings › Apps › Default apps › SMS app, choose Google Messages.

In Google Messages, tap your profile picture, then Messages settings › RCS chats, and turn it on. It can take a few minutes to say Connected.

Say yes when Ghost asks.

That's how RCS chats reach Ghost: it reads Google Messages' notifications. Only Google Messages' are read; every other app's are ignored. Turn it on for Ghost Messaging.

If Android won't let you switch it on, tap Open Android's page for Ghost Messaging, then ⋮ › Allow restricted settings, and try again.

Ghost reads them, hides each one as it comes, and shows its own. Turned off completely, Ghost would see nothing.

When every step is ticked: RCS is set up. Ghost shows every conversation, RCS and texts, and replies go through Google Messages.

On your computer

For full history, pictures and reactions on your Mac or Windows PC, open Ghost Messaging there, go to Settings › Google Messages, and scan the QR code it shows with Google Messages on your phone (Device pairing in its menu). Your phone needs to stay on and online.

Good to know

  • Google Messages' notifications are the data: if they're off, Ghost sees nothing new.
  • Some features of Google Messages may change without warning, since this relies on what it shows.
