Ghost Messaging is built so the people running its server can't read what you say. There are no ads, and the app is open source, so anyone can check.

What's sealed end to end

WhatSealed end to end?
Messages between friends, and in groups of friendsYes, with forward secrecy
Circles and spaces: chat, files, pollsYes, with a key the circle's members share
CallsYes, between the people in them: through Ghost's relay, or directly where the relay can't be reached, still sealed
Your texts with one person, with encryption onYes
Ordinary texts and picture messagesNo: they go through your carrier, as all texts do
Telegram, Signal, WhatsApp and Matrix chatsAs those networks seal them; they go between your device and the network, not through Ghost

What the server sees

  • Which installs talk to each other, and when, as any server passing messages would.
  • Your username, and your account backup sealed with your password (which it never sees).
  • Your verified number, if you verify one, to text you codes. It's shown to nobody unless you choose.
  • Never: what you write, your files, your recovery words or your password.

On your device

Spam filtering, sorting, translation, summaries, captions and transcripts all happen on your device. Your texts stay in your phone's own store. Plugins say on their pages what they can see.

Plugins from other developers are reviewed before they're listed. They never see your contacts' texts, group texts, encrypted texts or your own (and nothing at all while Ghost Messaging can't read your contacts, since it couldn't tell them from strangers), and they have no way to send anything over the internet: a button you press can open the plugin's page in your browser or copy words to your clipboard.

The Marketplace is fetched from our server, which sees your device's address and which plugins you add, and keeps no record of either. A report sends the plugin, what you chose and wrote, and your username only if you say so. A plugin you submit is listed with your username, help address and screenshots.

Blocking and archiving

  • Block someone from a conversation's menu or their card. Settings › Blocked lists everyone you've blocked, Telegram and Signal people too, and unblocks them.
  • Archive a conversation by swiping it left. Settings › Archived (or Archive in the rail) has them; a new message brings one back.

Forward secrecy

Messages between friends, and the copies your devices send each other, are sealed so that they stay safe even if your recovery words, your password or your account backup leak later. Each of your devices makes a fresh key every day and deletes it about two weeks later. A message is sealed with a one-off key that is thrown away as soon as it's sent. Someone who saved the scrambled traffic can't open it afterwards, even with your recovery words.

  • What it doesn't cover: the messages saved on your devices (whoever has your unlocked phone can read your history), who you talk to and when, and data texts. It also doesn't cover messages to people on an older version of Ghost, or the first message to someone you haven't heard from in a week; those use the earlier sealing.
  • If your recovery words leak, someone could pretend to be you from then on. Compare safety numbers if something seems off, and make a new account if your words were exposed.

In circles

A circle's messages are sealed with a key everyone in it has. When the owner or an admin removes or kicks someone, the circle gets a new key, which goes to everyone else and not to them. What's said afterwards in the circle and its spaces' chat can't be read by them, even with an altered app.

  • They can still see that people are talking, though not what's said. If they still have the circle's link, they can come back in under a new account, and everyone sees them join. Files spaces and voice-space call setup still use the circle's original keys.
  • Someone on an older version of Ghost can't read the circle after a key change until they update.
  • Leaving a circle doesn't change its key.

Leaving

  • Settings › Me › Remove everything about me unlinks every account and your number, and clears your watch list, on the phone and on the server.
  • Settings › Me › Account › Sign out of this device signs the device out; remove other devices from Signed in on these devices.
