Plugins are extras you choose: sort your texts, translate them, record calls, watch together. Some are on from the start; others you add when you want them. Everything a plugin does happens on your device unless its page says otherwise.

Add or remove a plugin

Open Settings › Plugins. Featured ones are at the top, then the rest by what they're for. Tap one to see what it adds and what it can see, then Add or switch it off. A plugin that's on gets its own section in Settings for its choices.

For a whole circle

Some plugins (marked Circle below) can be turned on for everyone in a circle, so every member has them in its spaces without adding them. Whoever may change the circle turns them on in Circle settings › Plugins.
