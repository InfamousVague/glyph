Record call records a space's call to one video file (MP4): first everyone together, then each voice on a track of its own, named for them. Edit one person's voice without the others, or listen to just one. Everyone in the call is asked first, and only people who say yes are in it.

Add it: Settings › Plugins › Record call › Add, or for a whole circle in Circle settings › Plugins. Works on Android, Mac and iPhone; not yet on Windows.

Record a call

While you're in a space's call, a Record button sits in the space's header, beside its settings. It's also in the call's menu.

A line in the space's chat says it too, so people on older versions know.

Tap Stop (in red, in the same place). The file is made on your device: it takes a moment. You're told where it went.

Where recordings go

  • On your device, always: in Recordings (from the call's menu), to play, share, save a copy, or delete.
  • In a circle, also into a folder of one of its Files spaces, for everyone in the circle. Until the circle chooses, that's its first Files space, in a folder named Recordings. If the circle has no Files space, or you can't add files there, it stays on your device and says why.

Consent and the law

Everyone is told and asked, and nobody's voice is in a recording without their yes. Laws on recording conversations differ by place; asking is on you as well as the app.
