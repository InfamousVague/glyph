A Ghost account keeps who you are: your username, friends and circles. With it you can sign in on another device and be the same you there, or get everything back on a new phone. Your texts aren't part of it; they stay in your phone's own store.

Create an account

Go to Settings › Me › Account and tap Create an account.

Ghost shows 12 words. Write them down, on paper, in this order, and keep them somewhere safe. Make sure nobody can see your screen.

I've written them down

Anyone with your 12 words can sign in as you, and without them (or your password) nobody, not even us, can get your account back. Ghost Messaging will never ask for them except to sign in.

Show you've got them: tap them in order, or type three of them. See the words again takes you back if you need to.

3 of 12

Pick a password, at least as long as the app asks. You'll use it with your username to sign in; it's stretched on your device first, so this takes a moment.

Create my account

Your account is ready. It shows your account's address (it starts with 0x). You don't need to do anything with it; How your account works explains it.

Or start from an account you have

Under Start from an account you have, sign up with Discord, Telegram or Signal. Your username, name, picture and status are filled in from that account, and nothing is claimed until you press Continue. It's offered in the welcome, and in Settings › Me › Profile until you have a username.

Sign in on another device

On the new device: Settings › Me › Account › Sign in to your Ghost account. Sign in with your username and password, or with your 12 recovery words alone.

Sign in

Signing in makes the device your account. Anything on it from before, as someone else, is replaced, and the app starts again. Your texts are not touched.

Devices, password and signing out

Everything is in Settings › Me › Account:

  • Change password: your backup is sealed again with the new one. Your recovery words stay the same.
  • Signed in on these devices: remove a device you no longer have. It's signed out the next time it connects.
  • Sign out of this device: the device becomes someone new. Your friends and circles stay with your account and come back when you sign in again.
