The relay server

Ghost's relay server (at gogho.st) passes sealed packets between apps: friends, circles, spaces and calls' setup. It can't read them; it sees only which installs are talking, as any server would. It's also where your username, your sealed account backup and a circle's sealed files are kept.

Your own relay server

Anyone can run the relay on a server of their own and use it in place of ours. Under Settings › Routes › Relay server, type its address (relay.example.com is enough) and press Check it and use it. Nothing changes unless a Ghost Messaging relay answers there; when one does, the app tells you what moving means and switches only when you say so. The section always shows which relay is in use, and Go back to Ghost Messaging's relay switches back in one press.

Friends on another relay

Relays can pass sealed messages to each other, so a friend doesn't have to use the same relay as you. Each of you goes on using your own, and the two relays hand each other what you write. Neither can read it.

To add someone on another relay, open Contacts, tap Add a friend, and type their username with their relay after it: @maya@relay.example. They are kept, and shown, under that name, and their card says which relay they are on.

Whether a relay talks to others is up to whoever runs it, and it is off until they switch it on. Settings › Routes says which yours is: Passes messages to other relays, or Doesn't pass messages to other relays. It takes both relays: where yours or theirs doesn't, the app still finds the person on their relay, tells you the request can't be passed on yet, and sends it when it can.

  • Crosses: friend requests and everything friends write each other, where both relays talk. Messages for someone who is away wait at their own relay.
  • Doesn't yet: calls, circles and spaces (each is a room on one relay), and who's online. The app says so instead of ringing a friend on another relay.
  • Kept on each relay apart: usernames (@maya on yours and @maya@relay.example can be two people), your account and its backup, invite words and Files spaces.
  • Add-me links look the name up on the relay you use, so on a relay of your own they may find someone else, or nobody. The app warns you before it sends a request.
  • Still works with anyone: texts (SMS), including encrypted ones. The Marketplace always comes from gogho.st.
  • Off on a relay of your own unless you set them up: calls through a TURN server, verifying numbers by text, linking Discord, X or Telegram. Closed iPhones can't be woken by your relay.
  • Someone on an older version of the app can't be reached from another relay: the app tells you they need a newer one.

So a relay of your own suits a group that moves together, for its circles, spaces and calls, and its people can still be friends with people elsewhere.

How to run one (Docker, or systemd behind Caddy or nginx, with coturn for calls) is in the repository: docs/SELF_HOSTING.md, with sample files in relay/deploy/.

SMS as a route

When the relay can't be reached, a message to a friend can go as a data text over the phone network instead: still sealed, needing no internet, and reaching a phone whose app is closed. Each one counts as a text on your plan, so only small messages go this way.

Test a route

Under People you can reach, press someone and choose Send a test, By the relay server only, or By SMS only (one text on your plan). Lately shows how recent messages went.

Invite someone by text

Invite someone by text opens a new chat with an invitation written. Choose who, change the words if you like, and send it as any text. Once they have the app and you text each other, it offers to turn encryption on between you.
