An open space is a call you walk around in. Everyone in it stands somewhere on one floor, seen from above, and you hear people louder or quieter by how near they stand. It is for a room full of people who want several conversations at once: walk over to the one you want.

Open spaces are built and being tried. They arrive in a coming release.

Make one

Make a space as you make any other (New space, or Add a space in a circle) and choose Open space as its kind. Joining its call puts you on the floor, near the middle.

Walk around

OnHow
A computerPress the floor once, then walk with W A S D or the arrow keys. Or press where you want to go.
A phonePress where you want to go, or drag your finger and follow it.

You stay in the middle of the view and the floor moves under you. The faint ring round you shows how far you hear: nobody beyond it is heard at all, and people get louder as they come nearer. People you hear less are drawn fainter, so you can still find them and walk over.

Who you hear

  • By distance. Someone beside you is as loud as in any call. They get quieter as either of you walks away, and come back when you walk back. Nobody is cut off or leaves the call.
  • Your own volume for each person still counts. Press and hold someone for their volume, as in any call. Distance works on top of it.
  • Someone on an older version, or a guest in a browser, has no place of their own yet: they stand in the middle of the floor.

Groups

A group is a named circle on the floor: one for the people playing a game, one for a film, one for a quiet chat. Inside a group you hear everyone in it whole, wherever in it they stand, and people outside it only faintly. From outside, a group is a murmur until you step in.

Stand where you want it and press New group. Choose Game, Casual chat or Movie night, or name it yourself. The group's circle is drawn round where you stand.

Walk into the circle to join it, and out to leave. The circle grows as more people step in, so there is room for everyone, and shrinks as they go.

Press a group's name to walk to it, rename it, move it to where you stand, or remove it. Anyone in the space can. A change is the same for everyone.

Groups stay with the space: they are there the next time anyone who has seen them comes back.
