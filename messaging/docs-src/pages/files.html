A Files space is a circle's shared folder: upload files, put them in folders, and open them on any device. Every file is sealed end to end before it leaves your device, so the server keeps only locked pieces it can't open.

Add files

Open the Files space and tap Upload. On a computer, you can also drag files onto it. Big files go up in pieces and carry on after the app is closed and opened again.

An upload that stopped part way and can't carry on (a file sent from a space's chat, or one from before the app was reinstalled) shows at the top of the space as Unfinished upload, with how much of it got there. It holds its room in the space until it is removed, or for a day after its last piece. Whoever started it and the space's owner can remove it to free that room, then add the file again.

Folders, moving and renaming

Press and hold a file or folder for its menu:

Move to folder Rename Share Watch together in the call Delete

Make a folder with New folder; folders can sit inside folders. Who may add, rename and delete files is up to the circle's roles: people with Share files can add, and moderators can rename or delete anyone's.

Videos

MP4 and MKV videos play in the app and start straight away, streaming as they play. Where a file has more than one soundtrack or subtitles, the player lets you choose. In a call with Watch together on, a video can play for everyone at once.

Sharing in from other apps

Share a file from another app to Ghost Messaging and pick a Files space to put it in. There needs to be a Files space first.

Sizes and space

A file can be very large (tens of gigabytes), and each space shows how much room it has left. Recordings from Record call can be saved into a Files space by themselves.
