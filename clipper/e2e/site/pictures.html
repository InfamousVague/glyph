Pictures from three places

This page shows pictures from three places, to see which a clip can keep. The first is on the page's own site, and is wider than a note ever shows a picture, so it is kept smaller. The second is on another site that lets any page read its pictures. The third is on a site that does not, and the fourth is far too big to keep at all.

Each of them is described in the words under it, and a clip of this page should keep the words whatever happens to the pictures. A picture that cannot be kept stays a link to where it is, so nothing is lost from the note.

That is all four, and the end of the page.
