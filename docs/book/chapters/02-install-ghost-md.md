# Install Ghost.md

Ghost.md runs on Android phones, iPhones, Macs, Windows and in a browser. Everything starts at the **Get Ghost.md** section of ghostmarkdown.com. You don't need an account for any of them.

## Android

1. On your phone, open ghostmarkdown.com and tap **Android**. The app downloads from the site.
2. Open the download. The first time, Android asks you to let your browser install apps. Allow it, then go back and install.
3. Open Ghost.md.

Ghost.md updates itself on Android. When a new version is out, a card on the home page says `Ghost.md 2.3.35 is out.` with **Install**. Tap it and the app downloads the new version. If Android asks, let Ghost.md install apps, then come back. You can also check by hand in Settings › About, under **Updates**, with **Check for updates**.

A Google Play listing is coming soon.

**Works on:** Android is the fullest version of Ghost.md: recording, meetings, the AI on the phone, files, scans and videos.

## iPhone

Get Ghost.md from the App Store. Search for Ghost.md, or follow the App Store button on the site.

The iPhone app updates through the App Store, like any other app.

The iPhone app is for notes you type. Some things aren't on it yet:

- No recording: no voice notes, no meetings, and no tapes to add to.
- No AI model on the phone. Claude, with your own key, does work on the iPhone.
- No files or scans in notes, and no exporting.
- The Notion and Slack plugins aren't on the iPhone yet.

Notes made on your other devices still arrive on the iPhone, recorded ones and meetings included.

## Mac

1. On ghostmarkdown.com, click **Mac**. It needs macOS 13.1 or later.
2. Open the disk image and drag Ghost.md into Applications.
3. Open it.

If macOS says it can't check the app the first time, choose **Done**, then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and choose **Open Anyway**. On macOS 14 or older, Control-click the app and choose **Open**. The site says when this step is needed.

The Mac app updates itself, from version 2.3.16 on. When a new version is out, the home page says so with **Install**. Once it has downloaded, choose **Restart to update**. Settings › About has **Check for updates** too. A copy older than 2.3.16 has to be replaced once by hand: download it again from the site.

**Works on:** the Mac has recording, meetings and the AI, like Android. On a Mac, ⌘ is the key for shortcuts: ⌘N makes a note and ⌘K opens search and commands.

## Windows

On ghostmarkdown.com, click **Windows**. It needs Windows 10 or later.

The Windows app isn't signed yet. The first time you run it, Windows says it protected your PC. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

The Windows app is an older version of Ghost.md. It reads and writes the same notes, but some newer features in this book, such as the Market, meetings and Simple mode, may look different or be missing there.

## In a browser

Open attack.fm/glyph in any modern browser. There's nothing to install.

In a browser, your notes are kept in that browser's own storage on that computer. Sign in to sync them with your other devices. Like the Windows app, the browser runs an older version of Ghost.md.

## Your notes on more than one device

Each device keeps its own copy of your notes. To have the same notes everywhere, make a Ghost.md account and sign in on each device, in Settings › Account. Sync is sealed: your notes are encrypted on the device before they leave it. See [[Accounts, sync and encryption]].

Without an account, everything stays on the one device. That's fine too. You can sign in later and nothing is lost.

## Which version you have

Settings › About shows the version you're running. This book describes 2.3.35.
