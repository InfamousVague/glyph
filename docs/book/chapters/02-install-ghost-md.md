# Install Ghost.md

Ghost.md runs on Android phones, iPhones and Macs, and an older version runs on Windows. Everything starts at the **Get Ghost.md** section of ghostmarkdown.com. You don't need an account for any of them.

## Android

1. On your phone, open ghostmarkdown.com and tap **Android**. The app downloads from the site.
2. Open the download. The first time, Android asks you to let your browser install apps. Allow it, then go back and install.
3. Open Ghost.md.

Ghost.md isn't on Google Play yet.

**Works on:** Android is the fullest version of Ghost.md: recording, meetings, the AI on the phone, files, scans and videos.

### Updates on Android

Ghost.md updates itself on Android.

1. When a new version is out, a card on the home page says, for example, `Ghost.md 2.3.35 is out.` with **Install**.
2. Tap **Install**. The card shows how far the download has got.
3. Android's installer opens. If Android asks, let Ghost.md install apps, then come back and tap **Install** again.

The app checks that each download was signed by us before it hands it to Android. If an install fails, the card says why, with **Try again**.

You can also check by hand: Settings › About, under **Updates**, with **Check for updates**. Under **Alerts**, **Update alerts** sends a notification when a new version is out, even while Ghost.md is closed.

## iPhone

Get Ghost.md from the App Store. Search for Ghost.md, or follow the App Store button on the site.

The iPhone app updates through the App Store, like any other app. Settings › About says so, and has no Check for updates.

The iPhone app is for notes you type. Some things aren't on it yet:

- No recording: no voice notes, no meetings recorded on the phone, and no tapes to add to.
- No AI model on the phone. Claude, with your own key, does work on the iPhone.
- No files or scans in notes, no export, import or backup, and no folder of your own for the library.
- No location: new notes aren't tagged with where you were.
- The Notion, Slack, Discord, Google Meet and Zoom plugins aren't on the iPhone yet, nor Place reminders or the plugins that bring notes in from other to-do apps. The Market says where each plugin works.

Notes made on your other devices still arrive on the iPhone, recorded ones and meetings included.

## Mac

1. On ghostmarkdown.com, click **Mac**. It needs macOS 13.1 or later.
2. Open the disk image and drag Ghost.md into Applications.
3. Open it from Applications.

If macOS says it can't check the app the first time, choose **Done**, then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and choose **Open Anyway**. On macOS 14 or older, Control-click the app and choose **Open**. The site says when this step is needed.

**Works on:** the Mac has recording, meetings and the AI, like Android. On a Mac, ⌘ is the key for shortcuts: ⌘N makes a note, ⌘K opens search and commands, ⌘F finds in a note, and ⌘, opens Settings. See [[Keys and the command palette]].

### Updates on the Mac

The Mac app updates itself, from version 2.3.16 on.

1. When a new version is out, the home page says so with **Install**.
2. Once it has downloaded, the card says `Ghost.md 2.3.35 is ready.` with **Restart to update**.
3. Choose **Restart to update**, or just quit: the new version is swapped in, and the next launch is the new one.

Settings › About has **Check for updates** too.

- **Run it from Applications.** A copy opened straight from the disk image, or from where it was downloaded, can't replace itself. Ghost.md opens the disk image in your browser instead, for you to install by hand.
- **Not mid-recording.** An update waits while a meeting or a voice note is being recorded.
- **Older copies.** A copy older than 2.3.16 has to be replaced once by hand: download it again from the site.

## Windows

On ghostmarkdown.com, click **Windows**. It needs Windows 10 or later.

The Windows app isn't signed yet. The first time you run it, Windows says it protected your PC. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

The Windows app is an older version of Ghost.md. It reads and writes the same notes, but much of this book, such as the Market, meetings and Simple mode, may look different there or be missing.

## In a browser

A browser version of Ghost.md is coming back. There isn't one to use just now, and there's no date for it yet.

Until then, use one of the apps. Notes you've synced to your account are kept in your account, so they're there on any device you sign in to.

## Your notes on more than one device

Each device keeps its own copy of your notes. To have the same notes everywhere, make a Ghost.md account and sign in on each device, in Settings › Account. Sync is sealed: your notes are encrypted on the device before they leave it. See [[Accounts, sync and encryption]].

With **Keep keys and connections in sync** on, also in Settings › Account, your Claude key and some of your plugins' connections follow you to your other devices, sealed the same way.

Without an account, everything stays on the one device. That's fine too. You can sign in later and nothing is lost.

## Which version you have

Settings › About shows the version you're running in large type at the top, with a line under it saying how this copy got there and where its updates come from. This book describes 2.3.35. On Android and the Mac, About also lists every release, with what's new in each.
