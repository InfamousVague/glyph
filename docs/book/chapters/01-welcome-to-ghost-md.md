# Welcome to Ghost.md

Ghost.md is a notes app for the things you type and the things you say. Every note is a plain Markdown file, kept on your own devices, and the same on every one of them. This book walks through all of it, a page at a time.

## What it is

- **Notes you type or say.** Write a note, or speak one on Android or the Mac and watch it arrive as Markdown, its to-dos as to-dos.
- **Plain Markdown files.** Every note is one `.md` file named after its first line. Any other Markdown app, from Obsidian to a plain text editor, can open it.
- **The same on every device.** Sign in and your notes sync between your phone and your computer, sealed on the device before they leave it. No account is needed to start.
- **More than notes.** Journals, notebooks, boards, tables, queries, tickets and canvases are all written in Markdown too, so they stay readable anywhere.
- **Meetings.** Record a meeting on your phone or Mac, or invite Ghost to a Google Meet call, and get a transcript and a summary of what was decided.
- **AI when you want it.** A model on your phone or Mac can tidy, summarise or fill in a note. Claude can work in your notes too, with your own key.

## The marks

A note is words with a few marks around them. Type `**Friday**` and it reads as **Friday**. Type `- [ ] Book the cabin` and you get a box to tick. Ghost.md draws each mark as you type, and the file keeps the characters you typed.

Ghost.md also has a handful of marks of its own, such as ==a highlight== and %%a quiet aside%%. In any other app they still read as the characters you typed. [[Markdown and the Ghost.md marks]] covers them all.

## Simple or Advanced

The first time it opens, Ghost.md asks how much of itself to show you:

- **Simple:** notes, lists and to-dos. Everything else waits behind More.
- **Advanced:** the whole app, with boards, databases, canvases, AI and teams.

You can change this any time in Settings › Appearance. Nothing is lost when you switch. See [[Simple and Advanced]].

## Where it runs

| Where | What you get |
|---|---|
| Android | The full app, with recording, meetings and the AI on the phone |
| Mac | The full app, with recording, meetings and the AI on the Mac |
| iPhone | Notes, lists, boards and the rest you type, from the App Store. No recording, no meetings and no AI on the phone yet. |
| Windows | An older version of the app |
| A browser | An older version of the app, with notes kept in that browser |

[[Install Ghost.md]] has the details for each one.

## Private by default

- **No account needed.** Without one, nothing of yours is on a server.
- **Sealed before it syncs.** With an account, notes, recordings and pictures are encrypted on your device. The service can't read them.
- **Heard on the device.** Speech is turned into words on your phone or Mac. The microphone is on only while you record.
- **No ads, no analytics.**

[[Privacy]] has the whole story.

## Learning by doing

You don't have to read this book in order. Two places in the app teach as you go:

- **How to format a note.** A new library starts with this note: a short tutorial, one mark at a time. Try each one, then delete it.
- **Ghost.md Academy.** Short lessons, a few minutes each. You type each mark and see it drawn as you go. Open it from Settings › About.
