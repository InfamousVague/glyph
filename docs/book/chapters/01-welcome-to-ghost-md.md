# Welcome to Ghost.md

Ghost.md is a notes app for the things you type and the things you say. Every note is a plain Markdown file, kept on your own devices, and the same on every one of them. This book walks through all of it, a page at a time.

## What it is

- **Notes you type or say.** Write a note, or speak one on Android or the Mac and watch it arrive as Markdown, its to-dos as to-dos.
- **Plain Markdown files.** Every note is one `.md` file named after its first line. Any other Markdown app, or a plain text editor, can open it.
- **The same on every device.** Sign in and your notes sync between your phone and your computer, sealed on the device before they leave it. No account is needed to start.
- **More than notes.** Journals, notebooks, boards, tables, queries, tickets and canvases are all written in Markdown too, so they stay readable anywhere.
- **Meetings.** Record a meeting on your phone or Mac, invite Ghost to a Google Meet or Zoom call, or ask it into a Discord voice channel, and get a transcript and a write-up of what was decided.
- **AI when you want it.** A model on your phone or Mac can format, summarise or fill in a note. Claude can work in your notes too, with your own key.
- **The Market.** Plugins, themes and fonts, added and removed in one place. See [[The Market]].

## The marks

A note is words with a few marks around them. Type `**Friday**` and it reads as **Friday**. Type `- [ ] Book the cabin` and you get a box to tick. Ghost.md draws each mark as you type, and the file keeps the characters you typed.

Ghost.md also has a handful of marks of its own, such as ==a highlight== and %%a quiet aside%%. In any other app they still read as the characters you typed. [[Markdown and the Ghost.md marks]] covers them all.

## Simple or Advanced

The first time it opens, Ghost.md asks how much of itself to show you:

- **Simple:** notes, lists and to-dos. Everything else waits behind More.
- **Advanced:** the whole app: boards, databases, canvases, AI, teams.

You can change this any time on the **Mode** card in Settings › Appearance. Nothing is lost when you switch. See [[Simple and Advanced]].

## Private by default

- **No account needed.** Without one, nothing of yours is on a server.
- **Sealed before it syncs.** With an account, notes, recordings and pictures are encrypted on your device. The service can't read them.
- **Heard on the device.** Speech you record is turned into words on your phone or Mac. The microphone is on only while you record. A meeting Ghost joins for you is sealed to your account before it's kept, and written up on your own device.
- **No ads, no analytics, no tracking.**

[[Privacy]] has the whole story.

## Learning by doing

You don't have to read this book in order. A few places in the app teach as you go:

- **How to format a note.** A new library starts with this note: a short tutorial, one mark at a time. Try each one, then delete it.
- **The examples.** Choose Advanced and a few more notes arrive to take apart: a notebook called Example project with three tickets, a board called Launch week, and a journal with its first entry.
- **Ghost.md Academy.** Short lessons, a few minutes each. You type each mark and see it drawn as you go. Open it from Settings › About, under Help.
- **The welcome walkthrough.** The pages from the first launch, again, from Settings › About.
