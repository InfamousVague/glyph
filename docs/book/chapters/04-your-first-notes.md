# Your first notes

This chapter covers making a note, naming it, finding it again, and putting it away. There's no Save button anywhere: a note is kept as you type.

## Make a note

- **In Advanced,** tap **+** on the dock (its label is **Write a note**). The **New** sheet opens. Choose **Note**.
- **In Simple,** tap the round **+** at the bottom. A blank note opens straight away.
- **On a Mac,** press ⌘N.

A blank page opens with the caret on its first line. Start typing.

The New sheet also offers:

| Row | What it makes |
|---|---|
| **Note** | A page of Markdown, typed or said |
| **Today** | Today's note, opened, or made the first time |
| **Entry in** a journal | A new entry in the journal you wrote in last |
| **Canvas** | Cards on a page with lines between them |
| **Notebook** | Notes in an order with an index, or a journal of dated entries |
| **Meeting** | A meeting recording, on Android and the Mac |
| **From a shared link** | A copy of a note or notebook someone shared with you |

In Simple, everything after Note is behind **More…**.

## The first line is the name

A note's first line is its name. It's what you see in your list, and it names the note's file too. Put `# ` in front of it to make it a title:

```
# Weekend trip
```

There's no separate title field. Change the first line and the note's name changes with it.

## Names and templates on a blank page

While a new note is still blank, a few things wait under its first line.

**Names.** Four ready-made names based on today: the date in words, the date as `2026-10-26`, the date and time, and the week as `2026-W44`. Tap one and it becomes the note's title. A name another note already has isn't offered.

**Templates,** under **Start from a template**: **A day**, **A meeting**, **A checklist**, **Notes on a book**, **A map at the top**, **A page to read**, **Bug report** and **Feature**. Tap one and the blank page becomes that note, with the caret where you write first. Changed your mind? Undo takes it back to a blank page.

**Your templates** opens a notebook called Templates, with the built-in ones as its pages. Change them, remove them, or add your own, and from then on a blank page offers yours.

The names and templates go away at your first letter. A note you leave without writing anything is never kept.

## Speak a note

On Android and the Mac, you can say a note instead. Tap the microphone (**Speak a voice note** on the dock, or the round mic in Simple), talk, and tap **Done**. [[Speaking a note]] has the whole story.

## Two ways to see a note

A note can show its marks, or hide them:

- **Markdown** shows the words formatted, with the marks still on the page, dimmed.
- **Formatted** hides the marks. The line you're editing shows its marks again so you can change them.

Switch between them with the first round button in a note's tools, or from the More sheet. Simple starts in Formatted. See [[Markdown and Ghost.md's marks]].

## Pin, archive and delete

Open a note's **More** sheet with the three dots:

- **Pin to the top** keeps the note at the top of your home page. **Unpin** takes it off.
- **Archive** takes it off the home page and out of your lists, without deleting it.
- **Move to Trash** takes it out of every list, with **Undo** for a few seconds.

On the home page you can also swipe a note, or press and hold it, for the same actions.

Notes in the Trash wait there for thirty days, then go for good. Until then you can **Restore** one, with its workspace and pin, or **Delete for good**. **Empty Trash** clears them all.

## Find a note again

- **Search.** In Advanced, the dock's **Search and commands** opens one list of your notes by name and everything the app can do. Start typing. On a Mac, ⌘K opens it too. In Simple, use the search field at the top of the home page.
- **The home page.** Pinned notes come first, then your notebooks, then your notes by when you last touched them. **All notes** at the foot opens every one.
- **Links.** Put a note's name in double square brackets to link to it. See [[Links, tags and footnotes]].

A note opens where you left it. Pinch with two fingers to make a note's words larger or smaller.

## Find and replace

**Find and replace** is in the More sheet. Or select some words, press and hold, and choose **Find**. On a Mac, ⌘F.

## Where you wrote it

If you turn on **Tag new notes with my location** in Settings › Account, each new note keeps where it was written. You can also add or remove a note's place from its More sheet, with **Add my location** and **Remove location**.

## Learn as you go

- **How to format a note** is the tutorial note every new library starts with.
- **Ghost.md Academy** teaches one mark at a time, in short lessons. Open it from Settings › About. If you know Markdown already, switch on **I know Markdown already** to learn only Ghost.md's own marks.
- **The cheat sheet** puts every mark on one page: Settings › About › **Cheat sheet**, or **Formatting cheat sheet** in a note's More sheet.
