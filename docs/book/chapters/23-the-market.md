# The Market

The Market is where Ghost.md keeps everything you can add to it: plugins, themes and fonts. Everything in it is built into the app. Nothing is downloaded, nothing is bought, and adding a plugin is a switch.

Works on: Android, iPhone and Mac. The Market is part of Advanced mode, so in Simple mode every way into it is hidden. See [[Simple and Advanced]]. The Windows app is an older version of Ghost.md with no Market: see [[Install Ghost.md]].

## Opening it

There are four ways in:

- The **Market** button on the dock, the one with the shop front. It sits on the home page's dock and on the dock of every page of your notes.
- The **Market** row in the sidebar, under **Tags**.
- The command palette: open it and type `market`. See [[Keys and the command palette]].
- **Settings › Market**, which closes Settings and opens the Market. Its row says how many plugins you have added, such as "7 of 16 plugins added".

Old links into Settings › Plugins land in the Market too, and a link to an old plugin page opens that plugin's page here.

## What you see

The page opens on a band in colour with the plugins' marks on it, and how many plugins, themes and fonts there are. Under it are a search field and four tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **All** | Featured plugins first (Claude, Notion, GitHub and Lovart), then the themes, the note fonts and every plugin. |
| **Plugins** | Every plugin, with categories to filter by. |
| **Themes** | Every theme, then the accent colours. |
| **Fonts** | Fonts for your notes, then fonts for the interface. |

On **All**, each shelf has a **See all** that takes you to its own tab.

The search field says "Search plugins, themes and fonts", and it filters every shelf at once. It matches a plugin's name, what it does, what it adds and where it works, so `iphone` or `desktop` finds the plugins that work there. A theme matches its name or its line ("Dark, with green"). A font matches its name or what it can be set as. Searching **All** also brings up the interface fonts. If nothing matches, the page says so and offers **Show everything**.

On a phone the Market is one column: featured plugins, themes and fonts scroll sideways, and plugins are a card each down the page. With more room, on an open Fold or a Mac, the cards stand two to four across, and a plugin's page opens beside them.

## Plugins

The Plugins shelf says how many plugins you have added out of how many there are, such as "7 of 16 added". On the **Plugins** tab, under it, are the category chips: **All**, **AI**, **Calendar & tasks**, **Import**, **Connections** and **Editor**. Plugins you have added are under **Added**. The rest are under **Discover**.

Each card shows the plugin's mark, a line on what it does, where it works, and **Add** or **Added**.

### Where a plugin works

Every plugin card says where it works, in a few words:

- **Mobile · Desktop**: Android, iPhone and the Mac.
- **Android · Desktop**: Android and the Mac, not the iPhone yet.
- **Android only**.

A plugin's own page spells it out, with Android, iPhone and Mac each marked, and "not yet" beside the ones it isn't on. "Desktop" here means the Mac app.

### A plugin's page

Tap a card to open its page. It has:

- **Add to Ghost.md**, or **Remove** once it is added.
- **Settings**, which opens the plugin's own page in Settings, once it is added.
- **Works on**: Android, iPhone and Mac, each marked.
- **What it does**: a sentence or two.
- **What it adds**: each thing it puts in the app, and "A page of its own in Settings" if it has one.
- **What it may reach**: your notes, the internet (with the sites it talks to), the model on your phone, or your phone itself, each with the reason. A plugin that asks for nothing says "Nothing past the note you are in".
- Its category, author and version, at the foot.

On a phone the page takes the screen: the arrow in the bar or the back gesture brings you back to the shelves. With more room, it opens beside them, and the cross closes it.

### Adding and removing

Adding a plugin turns it on. Removing it turns it off: it offers nothing until you add it again, and it keeps what it kept, so adding it back finds your settings where you left them.

Some plugins are on from the start: Claude, GitHub, Google Meet, Notion, Zoom, Marks and Rich embeds. The others wait until you add them, usually because they send something off your device, cost you credits, or ask your phone for a permission.

The switches for Claude, Discord, GitHub, Google Meet, Lovart, Slack and Zoom follow your account: add one on your phone and it is added on your Mac too, at its next sync, while **Keep keys and connections in sync** is on in Settings › Account. The others are each device's own.

### Each plugin's settings live in Settings

The Market is for finding and adding. Once a plugin is added and has settings, it gets a page of its own in Settings, in the plugin's own colour, under the Market row. That is where you sign in to Notion, paste a GitHub token or keep your Lovart keys. A plugin's page in the Market has a **Settings** button that opens it, and the foot of its page in Settings links back to **its page in the Market**. A plugin you remove takes its page out of Settings with it.

In Simple mode the plugins' pages are put away with the Market, all but **Google Meet** and **Zoom**, which stay in Settings so you can still invite Ghost to meetings.

### Local only

**Local only** (Settings › Account › Privacy) keeps everything on your device. While it is on, every plugin that uses the internet is held off. The Market says so over the plugins: "Local only is on, so plugins that use the internet are held off until it is off." The **Local only** beside those words takes you to the switch. Those cards wear a **Local only** tag instead of **Add**, and their pages say "It uses the internet, so it is off while Local only is on." A plugin you had added stays under **Added**, doing nothing until Local only is off.

The plugins that never leave your device keep working: Marks, Place reminders, Contacts and Things.

## Themes

Each theme is a little note painted in its colours. Tap one to wear it.

| Theme | What it is |
|---|---|
| **System** | Follows the phone's light or dark (or the Mac's). |
| **Light** | Ink on paper. |
| **Dark** | Paper on ink. |
| **Dawn** | Light, with red. |
| **Boreal** | Dark, with green. |
| **Ember** | Dark, with amber. |
| **Sakura** | Light, with purple, its greys tinted rose. |
| **Meadow** | Light, with green, its greys sage. |
| **Tide** | Dark, with teal. |
| **Dusk** | Dark, with purple. |

A named theme brings its accent with it. After the themes comes **Accent**, "the one colour Ghost.md wears": the chosen tab, a link, a ticked box. Pick **Ink**, **Graphite**, **Red**, **Amber**, **Green**, **Teal** or **Purple**. Ink is no colour at all.

The same choices are in Settings › Appearance.

## Fonts

Each font card shows "Aa" and a line of a note set in it. Tap one to use it. On **All** the shelf is **Fonts**, your note fonts; the **Fonts** tab has **Note font** and **Interface font**.

**Note font** sets your notes' words and code. Any of the eight can be a note font:

| Font | Also |
|---|---|
| **Maple Mono** | A coding face, with ligatures |
| **Fira Code** | A coding face, with ligatures |
| **JetBrains Mono** | A coding face, with ligatures |
| **Inter** | Can be the interface font |
| **Noto** | Can be the interface font |
| **Plex** | Can be the interface font |
| **Atkinson** | Can be the interface font |
| **Lora** | A serif, for notes |

**Interface font** sets the menus, tabs and buttons. Only the four sans faces can be one: Inter, Noto, Plex and Atkinson.

## The Market on the website

ghostmarkdown.com has the Market too, at ghostmarkdown.com/market.html: **Browse the Market** on the home page's Plugins band goes there. It lists the same plugins, themes and fonts, with the same tabs, categories and search, and each has a page of its own saying what it does, what it adds, what it needs and where it works.

**Open in Ghost.md**, on any of them, opens the app at that thing: a plugin on its page with **Add to Ghost.md**, a theme at the head of the Themes tab with **Use this theme**, a font at the head of the Fonts tab with **Use for notes** (and **Use for the interface** for the four sans faces). If the app doesn't answer, the page offers **Get the app**.

The links work from anywhere, not just the website. They look like this:

```
ghostmd://market/plugin/notion
ghostmd://market/theme/dusk
ghostmd://market/font/lora
```

`ghostmd://market/theme` alone opens the Themes tab, and `ghostmd://market` opens the front. A link to something your copy of Ghost.md doesn't have, perhaps because the website lists a newer version's, opens the front with a line saying so.

In Simple mode a Market link doesn't open the Market. A message says "The Market is part of Advanced mode.", with **Mode** to switch.
