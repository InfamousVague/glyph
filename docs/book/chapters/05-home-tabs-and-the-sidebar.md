# Home, tabs and the sidebar

The home page is where Ghost.md opens. This chapter walks through the Advanced home page, the dock beside it, the tabs along the top, and the sidebar. Simple has a quieter home of its own, covered at the end.

Works on: Android, iPhone and Mac. Tabs and the sidebar are part of Advanced.

## Four layouts

Go to Settings › Appearance › **Home page** and pick one of four. Each card is the page drawn small. The search and its filters stay on top whichever you pick.

| Layout | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **Spotlight** | Your pinned notes in a list, the four you touched last as cards, then everything else by when. This is the default. |
| **Cards** | Notebooks and notes as cards, each note drawn small. |
| **Timeline** | Everything by when you last touched it: today, yesterday, this week and earlier. |
| **List** | One line each: the name, how it starts, and when. The most on a screen. |

## What's on the page

Depending on the layout, the page is made of a few sections:

- **Pinned:** the notes you pinned, a line each. Spotlight puts them first, and doesn't repeat them lower down.
- **Recent:** in Spotlight, the four notes you touched last, as cards.
- **Notebooks** and **Notes:** in Cards and List, your notebooks first, then your notes.
- **Time groups:** in Spotlight and Timeline, the rest of your notes under **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Earlier this week**, **This month** and **Earlier**.

### The Meetings shelf

If you've recorded meetings, a **Meetings** shelf sits near the top: a row of cassettes that scrolls sideways, newest first, each with its note's title on the label. Under each is how long it is and the day it was. A meeting being recorded right now comes first, its reels turning. Tap a cassette to open the meeting's note. **See all** opens the rest. See [[Meetings and the Meet bot]].

The shelf doesn't show while you're searching, or in Simple.

### All notes

At the foot of the page is **All notes · N**, where N is how many notes you have. Tap it to see every one. The Cards layout stops after 48 cards; when it does, the foot says how many more are waiting in All notes.

## Search and filters

The field at the top says **Search notebooks and notes**. Start typing and the page shows what it **Found**, in whichever layout you're in. Archived notes stay out unless you type `is:archived`. More search words are in [[Workspaces, folders and search]].

Beside the field is **Filters**. Under **Show** you choose **All**, **Notebooks**, **Notes** or **Pinned**. Under **Workspace** you choose one workspace, or **Every workspace**. The same panel has **New workspace** and, where you can make one, **New organization**.

## The dock

The dock is the column of round buttons in the bottom corner. From top to bottom:

1. **Claude**, when Claude is on. With Talk to Claude, its microphone takes the place of Speak below, so there's only ever one microphone. See [[Claude in Ghost.md]].
2. **Search and commands:** your notes by name and everything the app can do, in one list. See [[Keys and the command palette]].
3. **Write a note:** the + that opens the New sheet.
4. **Speak a voice note:** the microphone, on Android and the Mac. See [[Speaking your notes]].
5. **Market:** plugins, themes and fonts. See [[The Market]].
6. **Settings**, at the foot.

## The New sheet

**Write a note** opens a sheet called **New**:

| Row | What it makes |
|---|---|
| **Note** | A page of markdown, typed or said. |
| **Today** | Today's note, opened, or made the first time. |
| **Entry in** a journal | A new entry in the journal you wrote in last. |
| **Canvas** | Cards on a page with lines between them. |
| **Notebook** | Notes in an order with an index, or a journal of dated entries. |
| **Meeting** | Record a meeting. The screen can go off. It is written up afterwards. On Android and the Mac. |
| **From a shared link** | A copy of a note or notebook someone shared with you. |

## A blank page

A new note that's still blank offers name chips based on today's date, **Start from a template**, and **Your templates**. They go away at your first letter. See [[Your first notes]].

## Tabs

Every note you open gets a tab along the top. Up to **8** stay open. Open a ninth and the one you opened longest ago drops off. Closing a tab never deletes the note.

- **Tap** a tab to switch notes.
- **Drag** a tab to move it.
- **Hold** a tab and let go, or **right-click** it on a Mac, for its menu: **Rename**, **Add to a new group**, **Add to group**, **Remove from group**, **Close tab** and **Delete note**.

### Tab groups

A tab group puts a name and a colour over a run of tabs. A new group is called "Group", ready to rename, in a colour no other group is wearing. Tap the group's chip to fold its tabs shut, and again to open them. Drop a tab between two tabs of a group and it joins the group.

Hold the chip, or right-click it, for **Rename**, **Colour**, **Ungroup** (the tabs stay open) and **Close group** (they close).

### The top bar's look

Settings › Appearance › **Top bar** lays out the controls and the tabs four ways: **Classic**, **Ledger** (tabs on top, as a browser has them), **Masthead** and **Islands** (glass capsules floating over the page). Your choice follows you to your other devices.

## The sidebar

The panel icon at the left of the top bar opens your notes as a tree. On a phone it floats over the note as a card. A tap opens a note in a tab, and a tap outside closes the card.

The tree has your workspaces and folders, with:

- **Tags:** every tag in your notes, and how many.
- **Market**.
- **Smart notebooks**, if you've made any.
- **Archive:** notes you've archived. Press and hold one and choose **Unarchive** to bring it back.
- **Trash**, at the foot.

Its tools along the top include **New note**, **New workspace** and **Search and commands**.

### Wide windows and the Fold

With room, on a Mac or a Galaxy Fold opened out, your notes can sit in a column beside the open note. It starts hidden. Press the panel icon to show it, and the device remembers your choice.

Settings › Appearance › **Sidebar** chooses how the side panel sits: **Popover**, over the note, or **Docked**, as a column beside it. This row only shows on a wide window.

On a Fold opened out, menus keep to their own side of the crease.

## The Trash and the Archive

**Archive** takes a note off the home page and out of your lists without deleting it.

**Move to Trash** takes it out of every list. Notes in the Trash wait for **30 days**, then go for good. Open the Trash from the foot of the sidebar, from All notes, or by typing `trash` in Search and commands.

Tap a note there for:

- **Restore:** back where it was, its folder and all.
- **Delete for good:** asks once. It can't be restored after this.

**Empty Trash** deletes everything in it for good, after asking. A note in an organization's workspace is deleted for every member, too.

## Simple's home

Simple's home is one plain list with a search field on top, notes grouped by when, and two tabs at the bottom: **Notes** and **To-dos**. A round microphone and a round **+** sit beside them. There are no tabs along the top, no sidebar and no Market. See [[Simple and Advanced]].
