# Settings

Settings holds everything about how Ghost.md works for you. This chapter goes through it a section at a time.

## Opening Settings

- The **Settings** cog at the foot of the dock, in the sidebar's tools, or at the end of the tab bar.
- `⌘,` on a Mac.
- **Settings** in the command palette.

On a phone, Settings is a list, and each row opens a page. With room, on an open Fold or a Mac, the list and the open page sit side by side.

**Search** sits at the top. Type a word and it finds the setting, not just the page: "spoiler", "haptics" or "token" lands you on the card that has it. On a Mac, `⌘F` puts you in the field while Settings is open.

## Simple shows fewer

In **Simple** mode, Settings shows only what a new person needs: **Account**, **Notifications**, **Meetings**, **Appearance** and **About**. Everything else waits behind Advanced, and the search doesn't find it either. Appearance is never hidden, because the way back to Advanced is its first card. See [[Simple and Advanced]].

Some pages also depend on your device. They are noted below.

## Account

Who you are and what leaves your device.

- Signed out: **I have an account**, **Create an account** and **Lost the password**.
- Signed in: your handle and how sync stands, then the **Sync** card (**Sync now**, **Live typing**, **Keep keys and connections in sync**, **Password and recovery codes**, **Sign out**).
- **Your colour**: the colour you wear in every organization.
- **Shared links** and **Published sites**, once you have any.
- **Privacy**: **Local only**, **Link previews**, **Look up blanks online**, and **Privacy policy**.
- **Location**: **Map on a tagged note**, **Place names**, **Tag new notes with my location**.
- **Export everything**: every note, with its pictures and files, as one `.ghostex` file, optionally with archived notes, organizations' notes, recordings, and a password.
- **Delete account**.

See [[Accounts, sync and encryption]], [[Sharing and published sites]] and [[Privacy]].

## Organizations

The teams you're in. **Invitations** waiting for you, with Accept and Decline; **Your organizations**; and **New organization**. Advanced only. See [[Organizations and shared notes]].

## Workspaces

**Your workspaces**, to rename, colour or delete; **Organizations' workspaces**; and **Daily notes**, which says where today's note goes (**Where they go**), what it starts from (**Starts from**) and whether it sits in a notebook (**In a notebook**). Advanced only. See [[Workspaces, folders and search]].

## Notifications

- **What reaches you**: four switches, **Team**, **Claude**, **Summaries** and **Conflicts**. An invitation is always shown.
- **Mute an organization**, once you're in one.
- **On this phone**: **Phone notifications**.
- **Reminders**: reminders for to-dos with a due date, and **The day before**, where your device reminds.

## Meetings

Shown when your account has Ghost in meetings.

- **Invite Ghost**: your Ghost address, to add as a guest to a calendar invite, or a Meet link to join now.
- **Ghost in meetings**: the meetings it is booked into, and the ones to skip.
- **Email to a note**: the same private address takes mail and makes notes of it.
- **Recordings**: **Sync meeting recordings**, on by default.
- **Sorting**: **Sort meetings into workspaces**, which files each meeting into the right workspace's Meetings folder.
- **Post to Slack**, with the Slack plugin added.

See [[Meetings]].

## Appearance

How much of the app there is, and how it looks, moves and feels.

- **Mode**: Simple or Advanced.
- **Page**: the theme. System, Light, Dark, Dawn, Boreal, Ember, Sakura, Meadow, Tide or Dusk.
- **Home page**: how home is laid out.
- **Top bar**: the look of the bar at the top.
- **Accent**: Ink, Graphite, Red, Amber, Green, Teal or Purple.
- **Type**: **Note font**, **Interface font**, **Text size** and **Scale**.
- **Spacing**: from Tightest to roomier.
- **Corners**: round or square.
- **Code**: the colours of code, for light pages and dark ones.
- **Sidebar**: Popover or Docked, on a wide screen.
- **Motion**: **Animation speed**, **Ghostly typing**, **Blur at the edges**, and **Ripples while recording**.
- **Touch**: **Haptics**, where the device has them.

In Simple, the page is Mode, Page and Type's text size. Themes and fonts can also be tried from [[The Market]].

## Library folder

Where your notes are kept as files. **Where your notes are** lets you choose another folder, such as an Obsidian vault or a synced folder, and move the library there. **Good to know** explains what that means for sync. **Files and scans** holds **Read words in pictures**, **Read what is already in notes** and **Files in notes**.

Not on the iPhone. Advanced only.

## Backup

Every note onto a USB drive or SD card, as plain files. Plug in a drive and choose it.

Not on the iPhone, and not in the Mac App Store's version. Advanced only.

## Export

**Export everything** as one zip, to carry away. Its **Import** row brings notes in from an **Obsidian vault** or an **Evernote export (.enex)**, and from Todoist and Things while those plugins are added. Each import goes into a workspace of its own.

Import is on Android and the Mac. On the iPhone, export isn't there yet. Advanced only.

## Recording

What happens when you speak a note.

- **While recording**: **Stop when I go quiet**.
- **After recording**: **Review after recording**, and **Better words**, which cleans up the transcript.
- **Summaries**: the model writes a summary under a long recording's title.
- **Meetings**: **Include sound from other apps** (on a Mac, both sides of a call), **Tell me when a meeting is written up**, and on Android **Write up straight away**, which otherwise waits for charging or a battery above half.
- **Tapes**: your recordings' storage, and **Remove audio older than a month**. The words stay.
- **Voice log**: what the app understood, for checking a spoken command.
- **Watch**: shown when a Wear OS watch is connected, with **Send short watch recordings to Claude**.

On Android and the Mac. Advanced only. See [[Speaking a note]].

## AI

**Model**: which model runs on your device for formatting and summaries, how big it is, and its download. **Fill blanks on their own**: blanks filled without pressing Fill.

On Android and the Mac. Advanced only. See [[AI on your device]].

## Market

The **Market** row opens the Market, and says how many plugins you've added. Under it, each plugin you've added that has settings gets a page of its own, in its own colour: Claude, Lovart, Calendar, Contacts, Place reminders, Things, Todoist, GitHub, Notion, Slack and Webhooks. A plugin you remove takes its page with it, and each page's foot links back to the plugin in the Market.

Advanced only. See [[The Market]] and [[Plugins, one by one]].

## About

- The **version**, large, and where updates stand.
- **Updates**: where this copy stands ("Up to date.", "Downloading Ghost.md …"), **Check for updates**, and **Install** (Android) or **Restart to update** (Mac) when a new version is ready. On the iPhone, updates come through the App Store.
- **Help**: **Ghost.md Academy**, **The welcome walkthrough**, **Cheat sheet**, and in Advanced the **Specification** and **Examples**.
- **This build**: what this copy of the app is made of, handy when reporting a problem.

## Developer

Hidden until you unlock it: tap the version on About seven times. A countdown starts on the third tap. **Developer** then holds benches and **Reset** (**Reset local data**, and **Reset everything**, which removes the downloaded models too), and **Test results** shows the build's test report. You won't need these day to day.
