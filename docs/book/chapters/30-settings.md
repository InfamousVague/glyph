# Settings

Settings holds everything about how Ghost.md works for you. This chapter goes through it a section at a time, in the order the list has them.

## Opening Settings

- The **Settings** cog: at the foot of the dock, at the end of the top bar, at the foot of the sidebar, or beside the title on Simple's home.
- `⌘,` on a Mac.
- **Settings** in the command palette.

On a phone, Settings is a list, and each row opens a page. Each row's second line says how that section stands, such as your handle and when you last synced. With room, on an open Fold or a Mac, the list and the open page sit side by side, and Settings opens on **Account**.

To leave a page, use the back arrow at the top, your phone's back gesture, Escape, or a swipe to the right across the page.

## Searching Settings

**Search settings** sits at the top of the list. Type a word and it finds the setting, not just the page: "spoiler", "haptics" or "token" lands you on the card that has it, lit for a moment. Every word you type has to start a word of what it finds, in any order, so "dark" finds **On the dark page** but "ark" finds nothing.

Return opens the first result, the arrow keys move through them, and Escape clears the field. On a Mac, `⌘F` puts you in the field while Settings is open.

## What Simple shows

In **Simple** mode, the list is only what a new person needs:

- **Account**
- **Notifications**
- **Appearance**, with just the mode, the theme and the text size.
- **Recording**, on Android and the Mac, when the Google Meet or Zoom page is there. It opens on its **Meetings** card, with the rest behind **More**.
- **Google Meet** and **Zoom**, while those plugins are added and your account has Ghost in meetings. Not on the iPhone.
- **About**, whose Help keeps the **Cheat sheet**.

Everything else waits behind Advanced, and the search doesn't find it either. Appearance is never hidden, because the way back to Advanced is its first card. See [[Simple and Advanced]].

Some sections also depend on your device. They are noted below.

## Account

Who you are and what leaves your device.

**Signed out**, the page offers **I have an account**, **Create an account** and **Lost the password** (which takes one of your recovery codes).

**Signed in**, the top shows your handle, "End-to-end encrypted", and how sync stands. If a note changed on two devices at once, or some changes weren't sent, it says so here. Then:

- **Sync**: **Sync now**; **Live typing**, so a note open on two of your devices, or one Claude is writing in, shows the words as they're typed; **Keep keys and connections in sync**, which carries your Claude key, Slack and Discord channels and GitHub token to each device you sign in to, end-to-end encrypted; **Password and recovery codes**; and **Sign out**, which leaves your notes on this device.
- **Your colour**: worn by your cursor and selections in a team note, by your comments, and by your row in an organization's members.
- **Shared links** and **Published sites**, once you have any.
- **Email to a note**: a private address that turns mail into notes in your Inbox, with **Copy**, **Make a new address** and **Turn off**. It's also the address Ghost is invited to meetings by. See [[Email to a note]].
- **Privacy**: **Local only**, **Link previews**, **Look up blanks online**, and **Privacy policy**.
- **Location**: **Map on a tagged note**, **Place names**, and **Tag new notes with my location**.
- **Export everything**: every note, with its pictures and files, as one `.ghostex` file. See [[Import, export and backup]].
- **Delete account**: your account and everything synced to it. The notes on this device stay.

The Privacy, Location and Export everything cards are there signed out too. While Local only is on, a line at the top of the page says so.

In Simple: yes. See [[Accounts, sync and encryption]], [[Sharing and published sites]] and [[Privacy]].

## Organizations

The teams you're in: **Invitations** waiting for you, with Accept and Decline; **Your organizations**; and **New organization**, a team by name that you own and invite people to by their handle. Signed out, the page says organizations come with an account.

Advanced only. See [[Organizations and shared notes]].

## Workspaces

- **Your workspaces**: tap one to rename it, colour it or delete it. **New workspace** makes another.
- **Organizations' workspaces**: named and coloured after their organization. One goes when you leave its organization.
- **Daily notes**: a page of its own. **Where they go** sets the workspace or folder today's note is filed in, **Starts from** sets the template it's filled from, and **In a notebook** puts each daily note on a notebook's index.

Advanced only. See [[Workspaces, folders and search]] and [[Notebooks and journals]].

## Notifications

- **Reminders**, on Android: **Remind me** for to-dos and tickets with a due date, the **Time** a due date with no time of its own reminds you, and **The day before**. If Android isn't showing Ghost.md's notifications, or may send them late, it says so, with **Allow notifications** or **Allow exact times**.
- **What reaches you**: four switches, **Team**, **Claude**, **Summaries** and **Conflicts**. An invitation to an organization always arrives.
- **Mute an organization**, once you're in one. Its team news isn't shown or counted; its invitations still are.
- **On this phone**, on Android: **Phone notifications**, so the bell's news reaches you while Ghost.md is in the background or closed.

Signed out, the page says notifications come with an account. In Simple: yes. See [[Reminders and notifications]].

## Appearance

How much of the app there is, and how it looks, moves and feels.

- **Mode**: **Simple** or **Advanced**, first in either mode. Boards, databases, canvases, AI, teams and most settings wait behind Advanced. Nothing is lost: switch back any time.
- **Page**: the theme. **System**, **Light**, **Dark**, **Dawn**, **Boreal**, **Ember**, **Sakura**, **Meadow**, **Tide** or **Dusk**. System follows the phone.
- **Home page**: **Spotlight**, **Cards**, **Timeline** or **List**. See [[Home, tabs and the sidebar]].
- **Top bar**: **Classic**, **Ledger**, **Masthead** or **Islands**.
- **Accent**: **Ink**, **Graphite**, **Red**, **Amber**, **Green**, **Teal** or **Purple**. It colours the few things that mark a choice.
- **Type**: **Note font**, **Interface font**, **Text size** (**Large**, **Larger**, **Largest**) and **Scale**, which sizes everything, buttons and bars too, and stays with this device.
- **Spacing**: **Tightest**, **Tight**, **Comfortable**, **Roomy** or **Roomiest**.
- **Corners**: **Rounding**, from **Square** to **Roundest**.
- **Code**: the colours of code blocks, **On the light page** and **On the dark page**.
- **Sidebar**, on a wide window: **Popover** or **Docked**, for the side panel that holds a notebook's index.
- **Motion**: **Animation speed**, **Ghostly typing**, **Blur at the edges**, and **Ripples while recording** where the app records. Your device's own reduce motion setting comes first.
- **Touch**: **Haptics**, where the device has them.

In Simple, the page is just **Mode**, **Page** and **Text size**. Themes and fonts can also be tried from [[The Market]].

## Library folder

Where your notes are kept as files. **Where your notes are** says where they are now, with **Choose a folder…** to move them into one of yours, such as an Obsidian vault or a synced folder, and **Use Ghost.md's own folder** to come back. **Good to know** explains what that means for each device and for sync.

**Files and scans** is here too: **Read words in pictures**, so a search finds the words in pictures, scans and PDFs; **Read what is already in notes**; and **Files in notes**, the room attachments take. Files up to 25 MB go to your other devices with their notes.

On Android and the Mac. Advanced only. See [[Import, export and backup]].

## Backup

Every note onto a USB drive or SD card, as plain files. Plug in a drive, and it appears under **Back up to** with **Back up**. Each drive you've backed up to shows what's on it.

On Android and the Mac, but not in the Mac App Store's version. Advanced only.

## Export

**Export everything** as one zip, to carry away. Its **Import** row opens a page that brings notes in from an **Obsidian vault**, an **Evernote export (.enex)** or **A .ghostex file**, and from Todoist and Things while those plugins are added. Notes from another app go into a workspace of their own, and **Remove this import** takes them back out. A `.ghostex` file comes back with its own workspaces.

On Android and the Mac. Advanced only. See [[Import, export and backup]].

## Recording

What happens when you speak a note, on Android and the Mac.

- **While recording**: **Stop when I go quiet**, which saves after four seconds of quiet once you've started talking, and **Review after recording**, a slower listen afterwards for recordings under three minutes.
- **After recording**: **Better words**, a larger model that goes over the recording and fixes the words.
- **Summaries**: which recordings get a summary under their title. **Meetings**, **Meetings and long voice notes** (over three minutes), or **Off**.
- **Meetings**: the same for every meeting, recorded here or joined by Ghost on Google Meet, Zoom or Discord. On Android, **Include sound from other apps**, **Tell me when a meeting is written up** and **Write up straight away** (otherwise it waits for charging, or a battery above half). On the Mac, **Record the computer's sound too**, for both sides of a call. Then **Sync meeting recordings**, signed in, and **Sort meetings into workspaces**, which files each written-up meeting in the Meetings folder of the workspace it's about. If some recordings stayed on this device, the card says how many and why.
- **Tapes**: how much room your recordings take here, and **Remove audio older than a month**. The words stay.
- **Voice log**: what the app understood from each recording, kept only on this device, with Copy and Clear, for when something went wrong.
- **Watch**, on Android, once a Wear OS watch has Ghost.md: each watch, and **Send short watch recordings to Claude**. See [[Android extras and the watch]].

In Simple, the page is its **Meetings** card, and **More** shows the rest. Otherwise Advanced only. See [[Speaking your notes]] and [[Meetings and the Meet bot]].

## AI

**Model**: which model runs on your device for summaries, the review, the AI in a note's More sheet and filling blanks. Each says what it is and how big, with **Get** to download it, a choice to use it, and Remove, which asks twice. Bigger writes better, and is slower. **Fill blanks on their own**: a few seconds after you stop typing, blanks the device can answer fill themselves.

On Android and the Mac. Advanced only. See [[AI on your device]].

## Market

The **Market** row closes Settings and opens the Market, and says how many plugins you've added. Plugins themselves live there now, not in Settings. See [[The Market]].

Under it, each plugin you've added that has settings gets a page of its own, in its own colour and icon, in the Market's order: **Claude** and **Lovart**; **Calendar**, **Contacts** and **Place reminders**; **Things** and **Todoist**; **Discord**, **GitHub**, **Google Meet**, **Notion**, **Slack**, **Webhooks** and **Zoom**. A plugin you remove takes its page with it, and so does one held off by Local only. Each page's foot links back to the plugin in the Market. Marks and Rich embeds have no settings, so no page.

The **Google Meet** and **Zoom** pages each have **Invite Ghost** for that kind of meeting (the same Ghost address works for both), the meetings Ghost is booked into, Zoom's **What the host must allow**, and a **Where meetings go** row that opens Recording's Meetings card.

The Market row and most plugin pages are Advanced only; Google Meet and Zoom stay in Simple. Discord, Google Meet, Notion, Slack and Zoom aren't on the iPhone yet, Place reminders is Android only, and Things and Todoist are Android and Mac. See [[Plugins, one by one]].

## About

- The **version**, large, and what this copy is.
- **Updates**: where this copy stands, such as "Up to date." or "Downloading Ghost.md …", with **Check for updates**, and **Install** on Android or **Restart to update** on the Mac when a new version is ready. On the iPhone and from the Mac App Store, updates come through the App Store; from the Play Store, through the Play Store.
- **Help**: **Ghost.md Academy**, **The welcome walkthrough** and the **Cheat sheet**. In Advanced, also the **Specification** and **Examples** (a sample note, a board and two canvases to add). **Ghost.md: The Guide**, the whole app as a notebook added to your notes, except on the iPhone and in the Mac App Store's version.
- **What's new**: every update, newest first, the one you're on marked. Not from the App Store.
- **This build**: what this copy of the app is made of, handy when reporting a problem.

In Simple: yes.

## Developer

Hidden until you unlock it: tap the version on About seven times. A countdown starts on the third tap. **Developer** then holds a **Work in progress** switch for the **Voice assistant**, readings of the **Window**, a scene to play, the **Developer settings** switch that hides the page again, and **Reset**: **Reset local data**, and **Reset everything**, which removes the downloaded models too. Each reset takes two taps and reopens the welcome walkthrough. **Test results** shows the build's test report. You won't need these day to day.

## In the browser

The browser version of Ghost.md is coming back. When it does, its Settings leaves out what a web page can't do: Library folder, Backup, Export and Import, Recording and AI. Its About says you're on the web version, which updates when you reload the page.
