# Simple and Advanced

Ghost.md can do a lot. You don't have to see all of it on day one. It has two modes:

- **Simple:** notes, lists and to-dos. Everything else waits behind More.
- **Advanced:** the whole app: boards, databases, canvases, AI, teams.

Both modes read and write the same notes. A board you made in Advanced is still in the note when you switch to Simple. It's just one tap further away.

## Choosing

The welcome walkthrough asks the first time Ghost.md opens. Its page is called **How much of it?** and offers **Simple** and **Advanced**. Simple is lit before you pick, and a tap applies your choice at once.

If you used Ghost.md before the modes existed, a card on the home page asks once: **Simple or advanced?**

To change your mind later, go to Settings › Appearance. The first card there is **Mode**. Switch any time: nothing is lost. Your choice syncs to your other devices.

If you never choose, Ghost.md runs as Advanced.

## Simple, at a glance

### The home page

Simple's home is a plain list of your notes, with a search field at the top and a Settings cog beside the title.

- Notes come in groups: **Pinned**, **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Earlier this week**, **This month** and **Earlier**.
- Swipe a note right to pin or unpin it. Swipe left to **Delete**. Press and hold for more, including Archive.
- At the bottom are two tabs, **Notes** and **To-dos**, and two round buttons: a microphone to speak a note, and **+** for a new note. Tap **+** and a blank note opens. Press and hold it for the New sheet, with a canvas, a notebook, a meeting and the rest behind **More…**.

### The To-dos tab

Every open to-do from every note, in one place, sorted by when it's due: **Overdue**, **Today**, **Later** and **Someday**. Tick one here and it's ticked in its note. What you tick in this visit waits on a **Done** shelf underneath, in case you change your mind.

To make a to-do, start any line in any note with `- [ ] `. See [[Lists, to-dos and tickets]].

### A note

A note in Simple has a short header: back to your notes, the three dots for More, and **Done**. Notes open formatted, with the marks hidden until you edit a line. A format bar gives you Bold, Italic, Heading, List, To-do and Link, then **Add image**, **Add to this line** and, on Android and the Mac, a microphone to speak into the note. On a phone the bar sits just above the keyboard while you type.

Only one note is open at a time. There are no tabs and no top bar. On a wide window, such as a Mac or an opened Fold, your list of notes stays in a column beside the note, with **Notes** and **To-dos** under its title.

### The + beside a line

Rest on an empty line and a **+** comes up in the margin. In Simple it offers the basics first: **A picture**, **A to-do**, **A heading**, **A bulleted list**, **A numbered list**, **A quote**, **A table**, **A link** and **A divider**. **More** holds dates, boards and the rest.

### The More sheet

A note's three dots open a short list: the view switch, Find and replace, Pin to the top, Archive, the formatting cheat sheet and Move to Trash. **More…** opens the rest in place: bookmarks, links, comments, the AI, sharing and filing. **Less** folds it away again.

### Settings

Simple's Settings has **Account**, **Notifications**, **Appearance** and **About**. Appearance has the Mode, the page's theme and the text size. While the Google Meet or Zoom plugin is added (both are at first) and your account has Ghost in meetings, their pages stay too, with **Recording** for where meetings go; the rest of Recording is behind **More**. See [[Settings]].

### The Market

Simple has no Market. A link to something in the Market, such as **Open in Ghost.md** on the website's Market, doesn't open it. A message says "The Market is part of Advanced mode.", with **Mode** to take you to the switch.

## What Advanced adds

Switch to Advanced and the rest comes into view:

- **A fuller home page**, with notebooks, a shelf of meeting tapes, filters by workspace, and a choice of layouts.
- **The dock**, with search and commands, a new note, a voice note, the Market and Settings, and Claude when you've set it up.
- **Tabs, the sidebar and the command palette.** Keep up to eight notes open, group tabs, and reach anything with ⌘K on a Mac.
- **Boards, databases, canvases and notebooks**, and every row of the **+** on its first page.
- **The AI and Claude.**
- **Organizations and workspaces**, for keeping work apart and writing with a team.
- **The Market**, where plugins, themes and fonts live.
- **Every setting**, including Organizations, Workspaces, Daily notes, Library folder, Backup, Export, Import, Recording, AI, and each added plugin's own page.

A new library in Simple starts with one note, **How to format a note**. Choose Advanced and a few more examples arrive: a notebook of tickets, a board and a journal.

## Which one should I pick?

Pick Simple if you want a quick place for notes, lists and to-dos, and nothing else in the way. Pick Advanced if you plan with boards and tickets, record meetings, use the AI, or work with a team.

Not sure? Start with Simple. Anything you need is behind More, and Advanced is one switch away.
